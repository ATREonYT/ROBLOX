## 1. What recent front-page hits look like (2025–2026), and what's trending now

### Takeaway
The 2025–26 mega-hits (Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, Plants vs Brainrots, Steal an Egg, Grow a Garden 2) aren't detailed environments. They're **simple, flat, bright-midday maps** with very little fog. Most of the color comes from the **collectibles** (crops, brainrots, eggs, pets) and their rarity or mutation glows, from color-coded bases and biomes, and from **frequent event and weather re-skins** that re-palette the whole map. Their layout leads the eye to a central lane or hub. Readability comes from big flat ground planes, a light sky, and the most saturated or glowing colors being saved for the things you collect or click. The current charts (Sept–Oct 2026) are led by **Steal an Egg**, with Grow a Garden 2 and Animal Hospital as the big 2026 newcomers.

### Inferences
- **[analyst observation] Shared "front-page look" in 2025–26 hits** (Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, Plants vs Brainrots, Steal an Egg):
  1. **High sun, midday light** (no golden hour by default). The sky is saturated blue with white puffy clouds, and there's little or no visible fog or haze.
  2. **Huge flat ground planes** in light, saturated grass greens (lime to yellow-green). Plots are bordered by bright wood or fences, and the dirt is a warm mid-brown.
  3. **Low environmental detail and high collectible color.** The map is the neutral stage. The color budget goes to crops, brainrots, eggs, pets, rarity frames and mutation effects (gold sheen, rainbow cycling, glow and sparkle particles).
  4. **Color = information.** Rarity, mutation, base skin, biome and team are all color-coded (red laser gates, rainbow bases, rarity-colored Lucky Blocks), so saturated color is never random.
  5. **Big readable signage** on shops: chunky text, white or black outlines, a saturated backboard.
  6. **Constant re-palettes** through events and weather (rain, storms, blood-moon reds, seasonal snow and summer re-skins). The map "looks new" every week without rebuilding geometry.
  7. **A central lane or hub that draws the eye**: Steal a Brainrot's conveyor, GAG2's circular hub, Steal an Egg's linear biome corridor.
- **[analyst observation] Pet Simulator 99 and Bee Swarm Simulator.** Every zone or field has one dominant hue (e.g. Bee Swarm's flower fields are big tiles of a single flower color), so a player can tell where they are from color alone. That's a good model for Hood's map tiers (Block → Suburbs → Uptown → Hills).
- **99 Nights** is the counter-example. It's atmospheric rather than candy-bright, and its pull comes from a **warm light pool (campfire) in a cool dark field**. Hood can borrow this for a night or "block party" mode, not for the default daytime look.
- **Implication for Hood:** a vibrant "hood" map shouldn't paint every surface at maximum saturation. It needs a **bright, clean, light-valued stage** (sidewalks, sky, lawns), **candy-colored facades at medium-high saturation**, and **maximum saturation plus glow reserved for interactables and rewards** (gym equipment, shop signs, cash, tier props).

## 2. Color and lighting recipes for "vibrant": concrete values

### Takeaway
Vibrancy in Roblox comes mostly from **lighting and grading, not geometry**: (a) a **warm key light with cool-tinted shadows/ambient**, (b) **low haze with a light-blue (not beige) atmosphere color** and a high-enough Offset to keep silhouettes, (c) a **positive ColorCorrection Saturation of about +0.15 to +0.3** with Contrast about +0.1, (d) **Bloom tuned so only Neon and sky bloom** (Threshold about 1.0–1.1), and (e) the **Default tonemapper** (not Retro). Hood's current Block lighting does the opposite on several counts: **Saturation −0.05, beige Atmosphere Color at Haze 1.6, Offset 0.12, ClockTime 17.2**. That's a dusty sepia veil, and it's probably the biggest reason the map reads as dull.

### Inferences
**Diagnosis of Hood's Block look.** A beige atmosphere color (218,201,172) at Haze 1.6 with Offset 0.12 washes every distant facade toward tan. Saturation −0.05 removes color on top of that. The low 17.2 sun adds an orange cast that pulls reds, greens and blues toward brown. Bloom Threshold 1.2 at Intensity 0.18 means the 11 Neon parts barely glow. Fixing these four settings should give the largest vibrancy gain per line of code.

**Preset H1 "Front-page Day" (default for The Block).** I synthesized it from the sources above. It's tuned for warm key, cool fill, low haze, and pop only on neon.
```lua
local L = game:GetService("Lighting")
L.ClockTime = 14.5            -- high sun, slight side light so facades get light/shadow sides
L.GeographicLatitude = 35
L.Brightness = 3               -- team's Steal-an-Egg value
L.ExposureCompensation = 0.1
L.Ambient        = Color3.fromRGB(118,122,150)   -- under awnings/porches: cool, never gray-black
L.OutdoorAmbient = Color3.fromRGB(150,158,192)   -- cool blue-lavender shadow fill (cf. Island Jump 134,158,190)
L.ColorShift_Top    = Color3.fromRGB(255,238,210) -- warm sunlit faces
L.ColorShift_Bottom = Color3.fromRGB(120,150,220) -- cool undersides
L.EnvironmentDiffuseScale = 0.6
L.EnvironmentSpecularScale = 0.25 -- keep 49 Metal parts from reflecting gray sky
L.ShadowSoftness = 0.15
-- Atmosphere: kill the beige veil
--   Density 0.26, Offset 0.55 (keeps silhouettes; 0.6 in Steal-an-Egg, 0.65 in Laser Tag sample)
--   Haze 0.25, Glare 0, Color (196,228,255) pale sky blue, Decay (120,170,255)
-- ColorCorrection "Punch": Saturation 0.22, Contrast 0.10, Brightness 0.03, TintColor (255,252,246)
-- Bloom: Intensity 0.6, Size 28, Threshold 1.1   (only Neon + sun/sky bloom; white sidewalks don't)
-- SunRays: Intensity 0.02, Spread 0.3
-- ColorGradingEffect: TonemapperPreset = Default (never Retro)
-- Clouds (under Terrain): Cover 0.55, Density 0.2, Color white; Workspace.GlobalWind = (6,0,3)
```
**Preset H2 "Golden Block Party" (optional evening or event mode, keeping the hood mood but vibrant).**
- ClockTime 17.6. OutdoorAmbient (156,136,176) is the doc's lavender evening fill. Ambient (120,105,150). ColorShift_Top (255,196,150).
- Atmosphere: Density 0.3, Offset 0.4, Haze 1.0. **Color (255,190,160) peach, not beige**. Decay (130,110,210) purple. Glare 0.3.
- ColorCorrection: Saturation 0.28, Contrast 0.12. Bloom: Intensity 0.9, Size 32, Threshold 1.0, so string lights and neon signs glow.
- Turn on all neon signs, string lights and window SurfaceLights. This is where the "hood at dusk" vibe lives, as a deliberate contrast to the default day look.

**Color-budget rules** (synthesized from the value-first and contrast-of-saturation principles plus the Brawl Stars HSV spread):

| Layer | Share of screen | Saturation | Value | Hood examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stage / neutrals | ~60% | 0–25% | 75–100% (asphalt is the exception, ~V 40%) | Sidewalk, curb, sky horizon, trim |
| Facades and nature | ~30% | 40–70% | 75–100% | Candy-painted brick, lawns, trees |
| Accents and interactables | ~10% | 75–100% | 95–100%, often Neon | Signs, gym gear, cash, tier props, doors |

- Reserve **near-black (V < 20%)** for small details only: outlines, tires, window frames, the ring rope posts. It should never cover large areas. A large dark roof or road eats vibrancy.
- **Never use gray in shadows.** Shadow color comes from OutdoorAmbient, so a cool blue-lavender ambient makes the shadowed side of a red brick wall read as a rich purple-red instead of a muddy brown.
- Keep **one hero hue per map tier** (Block = warm orange/gold accent 242,182,50 is already in config; Suburbs green, Uptown blue, Hills orange), Pet-Sim-style.

## 3. Environmental richness that reads as "alive" without clutter

### Takeaway
Hits feel alive through **motion and color on the things players care about**: bobbing and spinning collectibles, glow and sparkle on rare items, drifting clouds, weather and events. They don't get there by covering every surface in props. For Hood, a street map needs **a rhythm of small dressing at every doorstep** (planter, awning, stoop clutter), **a few big animated landmarks** (spinning sign, hydrant spray, flags), **light ambient particles**, and **people and sounds**. Leave clean, light-valued ground between clusters so the scene stays readable.

### Cited Findings
- Roblox's stylized Laser Tag sample adds life with three cheap layers: **dynamic background clouds** (Clouds under Terrain), **foreground cloud particles** (ParticleEmitter, Orientation FacingCameraWorldUp, Size 100, Squash −0.25, texture `rbxassetid://10714362544`) and **floating dust particles**. It also uses **Neon parts at Transparency 0.6** for glowing skylights and Neon floor strips in team colors. — [Construct your world](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/construct-your-world) (mirror lines 3823–3975, 2276, 4070–4175)
- Dynamic clouds drift with `Workspace.GlobalWind`. Clouds Cover runs 0–1 and Density 0–1 (light/translucent to heavy/dark). — [Dynamic clouds](https://create.roblox.com/docs/environment/clouds); [Global wind](https://create.roblox.com/docs/environment/global-wind)
- **Highlight outlines** (useful for marking interactables in a busy scene) now have a limit of **255 simultaneous Highlight instances on the client**. — `content/en-us/reference/engine/classes/Highlight.yaml` lines 49–52 ([Highlight API](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Highlight))
- Adopt Me's 2026 Summer Camp shows what a live-ops "dressing pass" contains: lily pads and tall algae in the river, docks, cabins, leaf piles, and a regraded grass color across the whole map. — [Adopt Me fandom](https://adoptme.fandom.com/wiki/Summer_Camp_Event_(2026)) **[search summary]**. The Winter update added "twinkling lights to giant snowmen" and a "glowing panel". — [findingdulcinea](https://findingdulcinea.com/adopt-me-winter-update-features/) **[search summary]**
- Weather as a re-palette system: Plants vs Brainrots goes back to a "sunny" default after weather events. — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/plants-vs-brainrots-weather-guide) **[search summary]**. Grow a Garden 2 adds a day/night cycle. — [games.gg](https://games.gg/news/grow-a-garden-2-release-date/) **[search summary]**
- The "hood map" detail vocabulary that guides list: "cracked sidewalks, graffiti-covered walls, boarded-up windows, and stray shopping carts", plus landmarks like "a park, basketball court, dilapidated gas station, or bustling corner store". — [roblox-hood-games-maps.pages.dev](https://roblox-hood-games-maps.pages.dev/) **[search summary; low-authority source]**

### Inferences
**Motion budget for The Block** (cheap, client-side tweens; keep most of it off the server):
1. **Collectible and reward motion:** cash and coins bob ±0.5 studs over about 1.2 s and spin about 90°/s. Tier props get a slow pulse of their Neon trim.
2. **Landmark motion (3–5 in view at most):** a rotating "GYM" or boxing-glove sign on the gym roof, a spinning barber pole at the clothing shop, waving flags or bunting (a small sine rotation on each pennant), an **open fire hydrant spraying water** (ParticleEmitter, light-blue droplets plus a tiny rainbow decal; very "summer on the block"), and a lit marquee with chasing bulbs on the stage street.
3. **Ambient motion:** dynamic clouds plus GlobalWind; 1–3 pigeons flapping between rooftops; laundry lines swaying; leaves or confetti drifting near the park (Rate 2–4, Transparency 0.3); occasional cars passing on a loop with saturated paint.
4. **People:** the existing 8 ambient neighbors (`BlockBuilder.lua` line 429ff), posed at stoops, the court and the bodega. Give them saturated outfits that match each house's accent color.
5. **Sound:** a distant boombox loop near the court, birds by day and crickets at dusk, the gym's speed-bag rattle, and a 3D ice-cream-truck jingle. Sound is part of "alive", even though it doesn't show in screenshots.

**Density rule of thumb** (inference, consistent with the flat-stage look of the hits):
- Dress in **nodes**: each stoop, shop front, corner, court and gym entrance gets 3–6 props.
- Leave the **sidewalk walking lane and roads mostly clean**. About 40–50% of the visible ground should be plain light surface.
- Repeat the rhythm: every rowhouse gets a planter box, every second house an awning, every third a mural or flag.
- **Clutter test:** take a phone-size screenshot and blur or squint at it. If you can't instantly find the gym, the shop and the next stage, demote some props (lower saturation and value) or remove them.

**Leading the eye:**
- Put the brightest and most saturated or neon objects at **destinations** (gym door, shop sign, next stage gate).
- Use a **lane line or painted path** (e.g. lane-yellow #FFD23F footprints or arrows on the sidewalk) toward the next goal.
- Bunting and string lights strung **across** the street pull the eye down the street axis (one-point perspective).
- The GAG2 hub and Steal a Brainrot conveyor examples show hits centering the layout on one bright focal spine.

### Gaps
- There's no sourced, quantified "prop density" guideline from the hits. The numbers above are heuristics.
- I couldn't verify specific animated-prop practice in Grow a Garden or Steal a Brainrot beyond the summaries.

## 4. Urban and street themes done vibrantly: palettes and props for "Hood"

### Takeaway
Stylized games make cities vibrant by **painting buildings in candy or pastel colors with cream trim**, layering **graffiti and murals with bold black outlines**, using **neon signs and billboards**, and relying on **complementary color pairs** (blue/orange, purple/yellow, mint/pink). Realistic grit (cracks, boarded windows, trash) gets swapped for **playful street culture**: basketball courts, block parties, hydrant sprays, sneakers, boomboxes. For Hood, keep red brick as the identity but **push it to a warm coral-red** and **mix in painted facades**. Add complementary-colored doors and awnings, **use the long stage-wall street as a mural gallery**, and save cyan/magenta/lime neon for signage.

### Cited Findings
- **Splatoon:** "Urban art, comic books, and street culture influenced character and environment design". It uses "a palette bursting with neon pinks, greens, blues, and yellows" and "complementary colors (such as blue and orange, purple and yellow)", which "makes colors more eye-catching… and easier… to distinguish". Earlier stages were "mainly constructed from inorganic materials" with many horizontal and vertical surfaces. — [Nintendo Ask the Developer Vol. 22](https://www.nintendo.com/en-ca/whatsnew/ask-the-developer-vol-22-splatoon-raiders-part-2/); [lemon8 Splatoon aesthetic](https://www.lemon8-app.com/booyahh/7331971608804278789?region=us) **[search summary]**
- **Jet Set Radio:** "crowded, neon-lit Japanese cityscapes filled with billboards, screens, and graffiti-layered walls, alleys, and train underpasses… in bright, cel-shaded… color palettes". It's known for a "yellow-and-green palette" and a deliberately "vibrant, cartoony look" in a time of gritty realism. — [Game Rant JSR 25 years](https://gamerant.com/jet-set-radio-25-year-anniversary-art-graphics-influence/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_Set_Radio) **[search summary]**
- **Subway Surfers:** "a celebration of graffiti and skateboarding culture". Graffiti lettering uses "**yellow, orange, and blue, with black outlines**". The environment has tracks, trains, barriers, warning signs, benches, bins, billboards and graffiti walls. — [Sketchfab asset pack](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sub-way-surf-assets-e435b57e2d7f459182b4af0fc3e38540); [Subway Surfers fandom](https://subwaysurf.fandom.com/f/t/List%20of%20Icons,%20Splash%20Screens,%20Logos%20and%20City%20Icons) **[search summary]**
- **Fortnite (GDC 2018, Pete Ellis):** "what initially started out as drab, monotone environments became bright and colorful set pieces with lots of personality". Simplifying realistic textures "made the texture too noisy", so the team hand-painted them. Its "sugar-saturated palette made the game instantly legible across compressed streaming thumbnails and small mobile screens". — [Game Developer GDC video](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/art/video-developing-the-stylized-art-of-i-fortnite-i-); [PSU](https://www.psu.com/news/the-secret-genius-of-fortnites-art-style/) **[search summary]**
- **Brawl Stars:** "bold lines, vibrant colors… exaggerated features", designed for "readability on mobile screens". Each map has an environment theme that "changes the backgrounds, blocks, bushes, and music". — [RetroStyle Games](https://retrostylegames.com/blog/game-art-design-like-brawl-stars/); [Brawl Stars fandom Environments](https://brawlstars.fandom.com/wiki/Environments) **[search summary]**. The palette hex values are in section 2.
- **Roblox city RPs:** Livetopia's appeal includes "brighter and more colorful visuals" and house variety such as "cake-style… Mediterranean-style… dual-colors". — [Roblox Cities fandom](https://robloxcities.fandom.com/wiki/Livetopia) **[search summary]**. Da Hood lets players spray decals and graffiti on walls. — [progameguides decal IDs](https://progameguides.com/roblox/roblox-decal-ids-spray-paint-codes/) **[search summary]**
- **Official stylized team-color pair:** mint (88,218,171) vs pink (255,170,255) on Neon in Roblox's Laser Tag sample. — [Construct your world](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/construct-your-world)

## 5. Thumbnails and icons: how map vibrancy supports them

### Takeaway
Roblox's own guidance links bright, high-saturation color to fun or "dreamy" themes in icons and thumbnails. It also **requires video thumbnails to show authentic in-game visuals** ("Artificial Visuals" are rejected). So the in-game map itself has to be vibrant. You can't fix a dull map with a graded thumbnail. A vibrant map gives the team authentic, high-contrast captures at feed size.

### Cited Findings
- Icon guidance: "Bright, high-saturation colors for a fantasy or dreamy setting" vs "Muted low-saturation colors for a somber, moody setting" vs "Heavy color balance and contrast for a horror game". — [Experience icons](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/experience-icons) (mirror `production/publishing/experience-icons.md` lines 98–113)
- Thumbnails: **16:9, ideally 1920×1080**. "Avoid placing any essential text or elements at the **bottom**… it may potentially be covered by metadata like the player count." A "Comical theme/character with bright colors" is the positive theme example. — [Thumbnails](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails) (mirror lines 207–277)
- Video thumbnails must be authentic. Allowed: camera work, "Minor Post-Processing — small adjustments like brightness, contrast". Rejected: "**Artificial Visuals** — Graphics shown must be representative of the actual in-game visuals. Avoid artificially enhancing graphics beyond what a player will experience." — [Thumbnails](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails) (mirror lines 55–115; mirror commit 2026-10-02)
- Third-party 2026 advice: "muted, realistic palettes disappear". Use "bold, saturated colors with strong contrast between subject and background", "fire-engine red, electric blue, sunshine yellow, lime green", "a rim light or an outline around your character", "a single character… filling a third of the frame", and "three words or fewer". — [vizzbees](https://vizzbees.com/blog/how-to-make-a-roblox-thumbnail); [generalistprogrammer](https://generalistprogrammer.com/tutorials/how-to-make-a-roblox-game-icon) **[search summary]**
- Fortnite's saturated palette was described as "legible across compressed streaming thumbnails and small mobile screens". — [PSU](https://www.psu.com/news/the-secret-genius-of-fortnites-art-style/) **[search summary]**

### Inferences
- Build **2–3 "thumbnail spots" into the map**: a spot where a player in front of the neon gym sign, the coral rowhouses and the blue sky fills the frame, and a block-party street shot down the bunting axis. Make them reachable in gameplay so captures stay authentic.
- Capture with preset H1 (day) or H2 (block party). Keep the hero subject in the upper two-thirds of the frame because of the bottom metadata overlay.
- A per-camera ColorCorrection boost for captures should stay "minor" (e.g. +0.05 saturation over the in-game value) to respect the authenticity policy.

### Gaps
- I found no Roblox-published data linking map saturation to CTR. The "35% dwell time" figure in one search summary had no traceable study, so I excluded it.

## 6. Practical checklist: the 22 most impactful changes for Hood, in priority order

### Takeaway
Most of the gain comes from five changes to the lighting and grading block (items 1–5), which take minutes. Next come repainting with a candy-facade and complementary-accent system plus more neon (6–11). Life and motion follow (12–18), and polish, readability and live-ops come last (19–22).

### Cited Findings
- Hood's current lighting, palette and material counts are in section 2 (`BlockBuilder.lua` 459–473, `Maps.lua` 7–16, `TheBlockV2.lua` 20–31).
- The team's own brighter recipe (Saturation 0.22–0.25, Bloom 0.6/28/1.1, Offset 0.6, midday ClockTime) is in `/home/user/ROBLOX/steal-an-egg/EggGame.server.lua` 390–412.
- Official samples: cool-blue OutdoorAmbient (134,158,190) with ColorShift_Top (196,222,255) in Island Jump; Atmosphere Offset 0.65 and Neon strips in team colors in the Laser Tag sample; lavender ambient (156,136,176) for evening; the Default tonemapper "vivid colors and high contrasts". — [Customize global lighting](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/core/building/customize-global-lighting); [Construct your world](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/construct-your-world); [Enhance outdoor environments](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/use-case-tutorials/lighting/enhance-outdoor-environments); [Post-processing](https://create.roblox.com/docs/environment/post-processing-effects)
- Highlight limit is 255 per client. — [Highlight API](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Highlight)

### Inferences
The checklist is synthesized from the sections above, with concrete values.

**A. Lighting and grading** (biggest win, about 30 lines of Luau in `B.SetLighting` and `Maps.lua`)
1. **ColorCorrection Saturation −0.05 → +0.22**, Contrast 0.05 → 0.10, Brightness 0 → 0.03, Tint (255,245,228) → (255,252,246).

**C. Life and motion**
12. **Bunting and string lights across the street** at every stage boundary and over the court. The sway is client-side.
