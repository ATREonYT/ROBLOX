# Rare-Drop Reveals and Camera Cutscenes (for +1 Cannon Evolution rare shots)

Researched Oct 31, 2026. Labels: **[cited]** means a source says it; **[suggested]** is our own number for our build and is not from a source.
A note on the sources: the fandom wikis (sols-rng / sol-rng / pet-simulator) and namu.wiki would not load from the cloud session, and web search returned a lot of spam/mirror pages. Most per-game beat timings could not be cited, so they are listed under Gaps. Frame-step the real games on 30 fps video before copying any timing.

## How do hit games stage rare reveals (Sol's RNG, Pet Simulator 99, anime TD summons, Grow a Garden, Blade Ball, RNG sims)?

### Takeaway
The pattern that is confirmed across hits: a **colour-coded tease** that tells you the rarity bracket before the name card appears (in Sol's RNG, a star pull whose colour and sound depend on the rarity bracket). After that, the effect gets bigger in steps as rarity rises: more layers, then a unique cutscene with its own music at the very top, then a **server-wide or global chat announcement** above a high threshold, and finally a **lingering aura** that other players can see. Exact seconds per beat are not published anywhere I could reach.

### Cited Findings
- Sol's RNG: auras rarer than 1 in 10,000 get a "star pull" animation. The pulls are mostly blue. The source lists Undead, Permafrost, Stormal, Solar, Lunar, Exotic, Celestial, Galaxy, Arcane, Chromatic, Matrix, Hades, Comet, Lightning and others as having one. — [Item Level Gaming](https://itemlevel.net/sols-rng-all-star-pull-animation/)
- Sol's RNG has a "Breakthrough" effect: a cracked white screen after the star pull when the aura is rolled outside its own weather/biome. It is an extra layer for "this was extra special". — [Item Level Gaming](https://itemlevel.net/sols-rng-all-star-pull-animation/)
- Sol's RNG: the star colour and sound step up by rarity bracket. One aura's opening cutscene "starts with a red star that plays the 1M+ sound", and a later Era 5 change gave it "the 10M+ sound". So there are distinct sounds for the 1M+ and 10M+ brackets. — [rblxguide](https://rblxguide.com/games/sols-rng/auras/chromatic-exotic)
- The same top-tier aura's lingering look: orbiting glowing orbs at hover height, a bigger orb above, stars around the player, a ring-shaped audio visualizer around the player, a large disc under the player. All of it "pulse[s] in time with the music". — [rblxguide](https://rblxguide.com/games/sols-rng/auras/chromatic-exotic)
- Sol's RNG: "the game plays progressively more dramatic cutscenes" as rarity rises. Transcendent auras (above about 1 in 1,000,000,000) "get fully unique cutscenes with custom music". — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/sols-rng-aura-tier-list-every-rarity-and-drop-rate-ranked/)
- Sol's RNG: auras rarer than about 1 in 99,999,998 are "global", and the whole server is notified when someone lands one. The same article's tier ranges don't line up cleanly. — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/sols-rng-aura-tier-list-every-rarity-and-drop-rate-ranked/)
- Sol's RNG rarity ladder: Basic → Epic → Unique → Legendary → Mythic → Exalted → Glorious → Transcendent (plus event tiers Challenged / Challenged+ / Dimensional). — [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/sols-rng/auras)
- Approximate rarity bands from allthings.how: Basic/Epic/Unique are 1 to about 1/90k; Legendary about 1/99,999 to 1/850k; Mythic about 1/1M to 1/9.4M; the next band 1/10M to 1/92M; then 1/99.99M and up. — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/sols-rng-aura-tier-list-every-rarity-and-drop-rate-ranked/)
- Sol's RNG has a "Server SFX" setting that turns off the sound played when *someone else* in the server gets a rare item. So other players hear rare rolls, and players can mute them. — [Item Level Gaming settings guide](https://itemlevel.net/sols-rng-best-settings-guide/)
- Pet Simulator 99 Void RNG event: "Land something rare and the whole server sees it in global chat!" Equipped auras show "your rarest hatch at a glance" to anyone nearby (a lingering aura as social proof). Hatches come in regular, gold, rainbow and shiny variants. The free Titanic has a base chance of 1 in 3,000,000,000. — [BIG Games Update 78](https://www.biggames.io/post/pet-simulator-99-update-78)
- PS99 Void RNG adds combo streak luck ("each roll adds more luck to the next", with bonuses at 10, 100 and 1,000) and timed luck dice. These are luck mechanics, not reveal visuals, but they set how often big reveals happen. — [BIG Games Update 78](https://www.biggames.io/post/pet-simulator-99-update-78)

### Inferences
- The common beat order across these games is: **tease (colour and sound tell the bracket) → build-up → freeze/flash → burst → name card with odds → announcement → lingering aura**. The tease colour is the key readability trick: kids learn "red star = big one" without reading anything.
- For our cannon, map the beats to a shot: barrel glow (tease colour) → charge → short hit-stop → muzzle burst → name card "MYTHIC SHOT 1 in 50,000" → chat line → trail or aura that stays on the cannon for a while.
- PS99's "rarest hatch shown as an aura nearby" fits our "+1" loop well: the cannon keeps a small tier aura after a rare shot until a better one lands.

### Gaps
- No source I could reach gave **seconds per beat** for Sol's RNG star pulls, PS99 Huge/Titanic hatch animations, Anime Vanguards / Anime Defenders summons, Grow a Garden or Blade Ball. The fandom pages that likely hold this would not load. Next step: frame-step YouTube compilations ("all star pull animations", "huge hatch compilation") on 30 fps video.

- Blade Ball: no reveal-specific source was found.

## How long does each tier's reveal last, and how does it scale?

### Takeaway
I found no published per-tier durations. The cited scaling *rules* are: animations start at a rarity threshold (10k+ in Sol's RNG), new sounds unlock at the 1M+ and 10M+ brackets, global announcements start around 1 in 100M, and only the very top tier gets unique music. The durations below are **suggested** for our game, sized for kids and for a fast +1 loop.

### Cited Findings
- Animation threshold 10k+, with extra effects for mutations over 69k. — [Item Level Gaming](https://itemlevel.net/sols-rng-all-star-pull-animation/)
- Separate sounds for the 1M+ and 10M+ brackets. — [rblxguide](https://rblxguide.com/games/sols-rng/auras/chromatic-exotic)
- Server-wide notification above about 1 in 99,999,998; unique music above about 1 in 1B. — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/sols-rng-aura-tier-list-every-rarity-and-drop-rate-ranked/)

## How are camera cutscenes built on Roblox?

### Takeaway
Set `Workspace.CurrentCamera.CameraType = Scriptable` and drive `CFrame` (and `Focus`, every frame). Use TweenService for 2-point moves and a Bezier/Catmull-Rom path for 3+ points (CutsceneService), or play Moon Animator 2 camera tracks with Moon2Cutscene. Return control by restoring `CameraType`/`CameraSubject`. Letterbox bars and the skip button are ordinary UI you script yourself.

### Cited Findings
- Each client has its own camera (`Workspace.CurrentCamera`). Setting `CameraType` to `Scriptable` "gives you full control of the camera". `CFrame` is the main property. `FieldOfView` runs from 1 to 120 with a default of 70. With a Scriptable camera, update `Focus` every frame, because some visuals render detail based on distance from the focus. — [Roblox Creator Docs: Camera](https://create.roblox.com/docs/workspace/camera)
- CutsceneService (DevForum community resource by Vaschex): Bezier-curve cutscenes. The author notes TweenService "is probably the best choice for tweening between 2 points" but chaining more than two points looks poor, so it uses Bezier curves. — [DevForum CutsceneService](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/718571)
- A CutsceneService bug report: the `Completed` event fired *before* `CameraType` was switched back, which broke a user's script. Lesson: restore the camera yourself, after the event, and don't rely on the order. — [DevForum CutsceneService p.64](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/cutsceneservice-smooth-cutscenes-using-b%C3%A9zier-curves/718571/64)
- Moon2Cutscene (DevForum) plays Moon Animator 2 files in game. Camera tracks work; other effects must be scripted by hand. Animation events are not supported (as of Aug 22, 2024). All animated parts must exist in the scene. v2.1 added `:reset()`. `.new()` takes a folder (object map) as its second argument. Black bars were requested but not confirmed. — [DevForum Moon2Cutscene](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/v2-moon2cutscene-play-moon-animator-2-files/3001411?page=3)
- Many people ask how to export Moon Animator 2 files into a game, and older threads went unanswered. This shows it is a common pain point. — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-do-i-export-moon-animator-2-files-into-my-game-for-cutscenes/3665918)
- Other ways to make a first-person cutscene exist, such as Blender plus an OAE module. — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-create-a-first-person-cutscene-using-blender-and-my-oae/2985958)

### Inferences — suggested build recipe [suggested]
- A `RevealCamera` client module with `play(tier, cannonCFrame)`:
  1. Save `CameraType`, `CameraSubject`, `FieldOfView` and `CFrame`.
  2. Set `Scriptable`.
  3. Step a 3–4 point Catmull-Rom path on `RenderStepped` with eased time (Quad/Sine InOut). Set `Focus` to the cannon each frame.
  4. Tween FOV separately: 70 → 60 on push-in, 70 → 78 on burst.
  5. Restore everything in a `finally`-style cleanup that also runs when the player skips, dies or respawns. Restore before firing any "done" signal (this avoids the CutsceneService bug).
- Letterbox: two black Frames at the top and bottom, each tweening Size Y from 0 to 0.1 scale over 0.25 s (whole-pixel offsets only, per repo UI rules). Put the skip button bottom-right and show it after 1 s.
- Keep the player's cannon firing paused (or queued) during Legendary+ reveals so input feels fair. Give the camera back within 0.3 s of the end.
- For our repo: prefer code-driven paths (easy to tune numbers) over Moon2Cutscene unless the owner hand-animates a camera. If a Moon file is used, its camera is a raw sequence, so follow the repo's KeyframeSequence extraction route.

### Gaps
- I could not open CutsceneService's full API or the Moon2Cutscene docs page (only reply pages loaded). Read the GitHub repos before using either.

## How are Lighting effects used during reveals, and what are safe limits?

### Takeaway
Roblox has Bloom, Blur, ColorCorrection, DepthOfField and SunRays post effects. Effects parented to **Lighting** show for everyone, and effects parented to **the Camera** show only to that player. For a personal rare shot, put reveal effects on the camera (local), and keep any server-wide sky change mild.

### Cited Findings
- The post-process effects are Bloom, Blur (Gaussian blur of the 3D view), ColorCorrection (Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, TintColor), DepthOfField (blurs out-of-focus areas) and SunRays (a halo around the sun that follows ClockTime). Under Lighting, effects show to all players; under Camera, only to that player. Set Studio's Editor Quality Level to max to see them. — [Creator Docs: Post-processing effects](https://create.roblox.com/docs/environment/post-processing-effects)

### Inferences [suggested]
- Reveal recipe: create effects on the client under `CurrentCamera`. Tween in over 0.2–0.4 s, hold, then tween out over 0.5–0.8 s. Never snap.
  - Suggested peak values: ColorCorrection Saturation +0.15 to +0.3, Contrast +0.1, TintColor a light wash of the tier colour (no pure saturated red); Bloom Intensity +0.5 above baseline for 0.3 s; Blur Size up to 8 only on the Secret freeze frame (0.15 s); DepthOfField with FocusDistance on the cannon for the Mythic+ name card.
- Sky swap: clone the Sky locally, swap it, restore it on cleanup. Tween Atmosphere Color/Density alongside.
- Always restore saved originals on skip, death and leave. Effects that are left on are a common bug.

### Gaps
- The docs page I reached lists no default values or ranges. Check the API reference pages for each effect class before tuning.

## Kid-safe constraints (flashing, near-miss, odds)

### Takeaway
Industry photosensitivity guidance fails content at about 3 flashes per second covering about 20% or more of the screen, and is stricter for saturated red. Our house rule (at most 1 flash per second) is well inside that. Odds must be on the name card. Tease colours must only ever show the **real** result, never a fake "almost".

### Cited Findings
- Xbox Accessibility Guideline 118: a flash is a 10% change in luminance. It fails at about 3 flashes per second, or when the flash covers about 20% or more of the screen. Lower-intensity flashing kept up over time can also fail. Saturated red (R/(R+G+B) ≥ 0.8) fails at a smaller change. High-contrast striped patterns over about 20% of the screen also fail. Removing triggers is preferred over warnings. The guideline recommends testing with the Harding FPA tool. — [Microsoft Learn XAG 118](https://learn.microsoft.com/de-de/gaming/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/118)
- Roblox players have reported dangerous flashing UI on the DevForum, and news reported a Roblox game allegedly linked to an epilepsy death. Treat flashing as a real risk. — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/extremely-dangerous-flashing-ui-issues/1645143); [GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78238-roblox-game-allegedly-linked-to-player-s-epilepsy-death-developers-criticised-for-negligence)
- Sol's RNG gives players a toggle to mute other players' rare-roll sounds ("Server SFX"). — [Item Level Gaming](https://itemlevel.net/sols-rng-best-settings-guide/)

### Inferences [suggested]
- **Flashes:** at most one full-screen white/bright pop per reveal, and never two inside 1 s. Use fades of 0.15 s or longer instead of hard cuts. No red full-screen flash. Mythic's red theme uses glow and particles, not a screen flash. Sol's RNG's "cracked white screen" Breakthrough layer should be reworked as a slow fade if we borrow the idea.
- **Near-miss: NOT allowed for us.** Don't do these:
  - a tease colour that climbs past the real result and then "drops back";
  - a slot-style reel that slows on a Mythic and ticks to Common;
  - "SO CLOSE!" text on a Common.
  The tease colour must equal the real tier from frame one. I found no cited source that documents near-miss teases in these specific games, so this is a rule for us, not a claim about them.
- **Odds:** every Rare+ name card shows "1 in N" with real numbers, and the shop or luck page lists all tier odds. Paid luck boosts show the boosted odds. Pity counter if the shots are ever paid (repo rule).
- **Announcements:** show the player's display name, the tier and the odds, and nothing else. Give other players a mute toggle for reveal sounds.

### Gaps
- No Roblox-official numeric flash rule was found. We use the stricter repo rule (1 per second) plus the Xbox thresholds as an outer bound.
