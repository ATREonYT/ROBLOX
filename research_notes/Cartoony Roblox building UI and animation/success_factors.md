Research date: 2026-10-03. How sources were checked: the network blocked most sites (DevForum, news sites, Rolimons, RoMonitor, sec.gov and others). Two kinds of source were used:
- **[DOC]**: Roblox Creator Hub documentation, read in full from Roblox's official GitHub mirror of the docs (github.com/Roblox/creator-docs, `main` branch, fetched 2026-10-03). This is the highest-confidence source here.

## 1. Case studies: recent breakout hits and why they worked

### Takeaway
The 2025 mega-hits (Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, 99 Nights in the Forest, Plants vs Brainrots) share six traits:
- a dead-simple, number-goes-up loop
- RNG rarity and mutation surprises
- progress that other players can see (and in some games steal)
- a fixed weekly update and "admin abuse" event ritual, usually on Saturdays
- creators who are personally visible on TikTok and YouTube
- the Roblox algorithm amplifying early traction

They also faded fast. Roblox's own Q2 2026 results show engagement moving away from "2025-vintage viral games", and the June 2026 algorithm change deliberately rewards long-term retention over spikes.

### Inferences
- The repeatable pattern is not a genre but a combination:
  - a number that visibly goes up every second
  - RNG rarity tiers with rare "mutations" or "secrets"
  - progress other players can see (a garden, a base, a crew)
  - some light player-to-player tension (stealing in SaB and GaG)
  - a fixed weekly event ritual
- Hood already has number-goes-up (Power/sec), collection (crew/pet boxes) and prestige (rebirth). The missing pieces are visible social status and a weekly ritual.
- Small teams can break out (GaG's first version took days; Blade Ball went from 500 to 285K CCU in a month), but the 2025 mega-spikes were exceptional. Platform data shows the median DevEx creator earns about $1.5K/year.
- After June 2026, Roblox actively steers traffic toward games that retain over 28 days. The safer 2026 strategy is "evergreen simulator with weekly content", in the mould of Bee Swarm or Pet Sim, rather than chasing a one-month viral spike.
- Studio leverage matters. Plants vs Brainrots reached about 8.65M CCU partly because Jandel's team already had a huge audience. A new team should borrow audience through influencers, share links and events rather than expect instant scale.

## 2. Core loop and progression design for simulators (growth curves, rebirth, offline earnings, worlds, collection, dailies, onboarding)

### Takeaway
The proven simulator template is:
1. Earn per second or per click.
2. Spend on multipliers.
3. Hit a gate (wall, zone or boss).
4. Rebirth for a permanent multiplier.
5. Unlock a new world.

The hits layer on:
- offline accrual
- RNG rarity with visible mutations
- public display of progress
- daily and streak incentives

Roblox's own guidance stresses three FTUE rules: a brief tutorial, a "joyful moment" right after the first loop completion, and previewing future progress. Roblox's algorithm penalizes bounces under 60 seconds and between 61–180 seconds, so the first wall break must come within the first few minutes.

### Cited Findings

**Roblox's official core-loop and FTUE guidance** [DOC] — [Retention doc](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/analytics/retention.md) (canonical: create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention)
- Improve D1 retention through "core loop, first-time user experience, and performance". For the core loop: identify the core steps and make them clear and fun, balance the challenge, and reward completion "with feedback, currency, items, and achievements".
- FTUE:
  1. "Use a brief tutorial … Avoid long tutorials to help users get to the fun as quickly as possible."
  2. "Deliver a joyful moment after users complete your core loop for the first time."
  3. "Preview the progress that users can make if they complete your core loop multiple times."
- Track the completion rate of each FTUE step with funnel events. [DOC] — [Funnel events](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/analytics/funnel-events.md)

**Onboarding techniques** [DOC] — [Onboarding techniques](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/game-design/onboarding-techniques.md)
- Three techniques: visual elements (bouncing arrows on UI, particles on items, glowing trails to destinations, in-world signs showing the core loop), contextual tutorials, and timed hints.
- "Players who don't understand what to do in the first few minutes are likely to quit, but so are players who are bored by lengthy and prescriptive tutorials."
- Example given: A/B test a 5-second vs 10-second hint delay with **Experiments**, then roll out the winner live with **Configs**.

**Discovery signals that shape loop design** [DOC] — [Discovery](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/discovery.md)
- "First play bounce rate" is a **negative** signal, measured in two buckets: **<60 seconds** and **61–180 seconds**.
- "Playtime per user" is **capped at 60 minutes per user, per game, per day**.
- "Play days per user" is measured at D1, D2–7 and D8–28.
- **Takeaway:** many return days are worth more than marathon sessions.

**Creator Rewards (replaced engagement payouts on 2025-07-24)** [DOC] — [Creator Rewards](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/creator-rewards.md)
- Pays **5 Robux/day per "Active Spender"** (someone with ≥$9.99 spent on Roblox in the past 60 days) who plays **10+ minutes** in your game, provided it is one of the **first three experiences they launch that day**.
- **Takeaway:** this rewards being a daily-habit "first stop" game.

**Grow a Garden mechanics, as analyzed by Naavik** [SEARCH] — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/predicting-the-next-big-hits-on-roblox/)
- Offline growth makes returning rewarding.
- RNG mutations and loot packs create surprise.
- Daily incentives build streak pressure.
- Public gardens act as status.

**Roblox design guidance on Roblox audiences** [DOC] — [Design for Roblox](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/game-design/design-for-roblox.md)
- **Social:** users "act as evergreen sources of content for each other". Don't block social play with monetization; for example, don't make private servers prohibitively expensive.
- **Genre norms:** "The closer your design patterns and user experience are to the most popular games in that genre, the less you have to explain upfront."
- **Younger users** favor exploration, experimentation and socializing over competition. They consume content shaped by short-form video.
- **"Weirdness and creativity are a core part of Roblox culture."**
- **Streamers:** design for streamers; Brookhaven's "Creator Cam" is the example.

**Tourists vs locals** [DOC] — [Monetization index](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/index.md)
- **Tourists** hop between games and want items with immediate effects or visible flex.
- **Locals** engage deeply, form "almost all a game's engaged user base", and want long-term-benefit items such as battle passes.
- Many locals start as tourists.

**Content cadence that extends progression** [DOC] — [Content updates](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/game-design/content-updates.md)
- Games with "gated map zones" can use cadence updates to add content **at the end of progression** for veterans.
- Cadence content "should not be immediately consumed by the majority of players". Require earning it.
- Keep each cadence release under **3 weeks of effort**. Many games ship cadence content every two weeks.

**Rebirth and number curves (community guidance; no official Roblox doc)**
- Upgrade and rebirth costs typically scale exponentially. DevForum developers warn that compounding like `price * 1.1^rebirths` explodes (about 4 googol at 10,000 rebirths) even at 5%. [SEARCH] — [DevForum: rebirth formula thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/good-formula-to-calculate-amount-needed-to-rebirth/1047686)
- Recommended pattern: one table defining what resets, what persists and what multiplies. Compute the new state and save it before applying it live, to avoid data loss. [SEARCH] — [picoo.io rebirth guide](https://picoo.io/how-to-make/roblox-rebirth-system)

**Analog multiplier pass**
- Arm Wrestle Simulator's VIP pass **triples training earnings** for 249 R$. [SEARCH] — [Item Level Gaming](https://itemlevel.net/arm-wrestle-simulator-best-gamepasses-to-get/)

**Offline progress as a premium hook**
- Bee Swarm's Beesmas 2025 added an "Offline Voucher" that lets planters, the blender and other machines work offline for 24h. [SEARCH] — [BSS Fandom](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/f/p/4400000000000796259/r/4400000000003365281)

**Roblox discourages skippable timers** [DOC] — [Monetization index](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/index.md)
- "appointment mechanics and timers which can be removed or brought forward … are unpopular with Roblox users who take issue with their fun coming to a premature end."

### Inferences (applied to Hood)

**First 30 seconds**
- Spawn the player facing a training bag next to the first wall, with a giant Power counter visible.
- Every punch or tick should pop a floating "+X Power" number.
- Show the first wall's required Power as a progress bar ("12 / 50 Power").
- Target: the first wall breaks within about 60–120 seconds. That beats the <60s and 61–180s bounce buckets and is the docs' "joyful moment". Make it explode: confetti, camera shake, a Cash shower.

**First 5 minutes**
- Break the first 2–3 walls.
- Earn the first Cash and open the first free crew box.
- Equip the first outfit and see the multiplier.
- See (but not reach) the rebirth button and the next neighborhood gate. This "previews progress".

**Number curve**
- Use geometric wall costs: for example wall n costs about base × 1.6–2.2^n within a world.
- Rebirth should grant an **additive or slowly compounding** multiplier, for example +50% per rebirth or a tiered ladder. Avoid unbounded `x*1.1^n` stacking.
- Use number abbreviations (K, M, B, T, Qa…).
- Each new neighborhood should reset local wall costs but use a higher base.

**Retention hooks the algorithm explicitly values**
- **Play days:** daily login streak, a daily free crew box, offline Power/Cash accrual claimed on return (capped, for example 8–12h, upgradable), and daily or weekly quests.
- **Intentional co-play days:** a friend-joined boost (for example +10% Power per friend in the server), a "spar/train together" bonus, and crew battles.
- **Playtime cap at 60 min/day:** design sessions of about 15–30 minutes with natural end points (a daily quest set) rather than relying on endless grinding.

**Social status**
- Make crews and pets follow the player visibly.
- Put nametags above heads showing rebirth tier or neighborhood.
- Add a server leaderboard for Power.
- Make wall breaks visible to others (big FX and a server-wide shout for rare breaks). This mirrors GaG's "gardens visible to other players".

**RNG with transparency**
- Crew boxes should have rarity tiers and rare "mutations" (shiny or gold variants). This mirrors GaG mutations and SaB "secrets".
- Always show odds (see section 4).

**Ritual**
- A weekly Saturday update plus a 30–60 minute "Block Party" / admin event: server-wide boosts, a special box restock, an exclusive cosmetic.

### Gaps
- No official Roblox document gives recommended numeric growth-curve parameters for simulators. The curve values above are design inferences, not sourced benchmarks.
- No public A/B data on the optimal offline-earnings cap or rebirth multiplier size.

## 3. Retention benchmarks, the discovery algorithm, and thumbnails/titles/A-B testing

### Takeaway
Roblox's Recommended For You (RFY) algorithm ranks by per-user signals, counted **only from users who arrived organically via RFY**. The most important signals are:
- play-through rate
- first-play bounce rate
- play days per user
- playtime per user (capped at 60 min/day)

On **2026-06-15** Roblox rolled out a global change. It evaluates signals over a **28-day window** (buckets D1, D2–7, D8–28) and replaced "qualified play-through rate" with play-through rate plus first-play bounce. Roblox explicitly accepted lower short-term monetization to favor retentive games.

Benchmarks for large games (GameAnalytics 2026):

| Metric | Median | Top 1% |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | 10.3% | 22.2% |
| D7 | 1.6% | 9.1% |
| D30 | 0.5% | 4.7% |

Thumbnail personalization (2–5 active thumbnails) raised qualified play-through rate by +8.5% on average in Roblox's testing.

### Cited Findings

**How RFY works** [DOC] — [Discovery](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/discovery.md); [Discovery FAQ](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/discovery-faq.md)
- **Two stages.** Retrieval uses engagement, retention and monetization signals. Ranking is personalized. "Signals from sponsored ads, curation, search, charts, friends … can accelerate your consideration … Games that have even a small number of people playing can signal to the system…"
- **Only organic RFY users count.** "Roblox doesn't count the engagement, monetization, or retention of users first acquired from ads, curation, friends, search, social media, or any other source in the ranking stage."
- **Signal priority:**
  - Most important: play-through rate; first play bounce rate (negative; <60s and 61–180s); play days per user; playtime per user (max 60 min/user/game/day).
  - Important: intentional co-play days per user; qualified play sessions per user; spend days per user; Robux spent per user.
  - Time segments: D1, D2–7, D8–28.
- **Explore and expand.** A content update can produce a spike in RFY traffic (explore). If that cohort engages and monetizes, Roblox recommends the game to more similar cohorts (expand).
- **Seasonality.** Home traffic "usually peaks on a Saturday and lowers during weekdays". Summer and holidays also matter.
- **Relative ranking.** If competitors improve more than you do, your impressions can fall even when your own metrics rise.
- **Per-user averages.** Signals are "averages per user, not total values", so small games are not disadvantaged.
- **No permanent penalty.** "The algorithm tests your game daily … if it sees improving performance, it increases impressions." New games don't wait 28 days: PTR and bounce apply immediately, then D1, D2–7 and D8–28 follow.
- **Retention before monetization.** "Games that are strong in only one area … will continue to receive Home recommendation impressions," but strength in both gets broader distribution. "If retention is low, focus on core gameplay first. Once retention is strong, improving monetization can help you increase your Home impressions."
- **Things that limit exposure:**
  - metadata implying monetary giveaways (for example "Robux! Play now!")
  - metadata mismatched with gameplay
  - **"Non-unique games"** whose metadata or place files closely resemble existing games are "no longer prioritized for recommendations"
  - The Creator Dashboard shows a daily-updated banner if quality issues reduce exposure.
- **Other surfaces:** Continue Playing, Friends, Sponsored, curated sorts, Standout Games (hand-curated novel games), Live Events, Search (now semantic, for example "food games"), and the Discover/Charts page with top and trending sorts.
- **Notifications:** experience notifications and in-game opt-in prompts can resurrect lapsed users.

## 4. Monetization: passes vs products, price points, subscriptions, Plus, ads, regional pricing, and policy limits (minors, paid random items)

### Takeaway
Simulators monetize through:
- **one-time passes**: multipliers such as 2x/3x Power, auto-train, VIP, extra storage, luck and auto-rebirth, typically about 79–400 R$ with premium tiers of 1,200–3,250 R$
- **repeatable developer products**: boosts, currency packs, box bundles, skips

New in 2025–2026, with major effect:
- Engagement payouts replaced by **Creator Rewards** (2025-07)
- **Regional pricing**, then **Managed Pricing**
- in-game **subscriptions** priced in Robux
- **Roblox Plus** replacing Premium on 2026-04-30
- **rewarded video ads**

Policy is now strict:
- Any random outcome bought directly or indirectly with Robux needs itemized percentage odds before purchase.
- Paid random items and paid-item trading must be gated with PolicyService.
- No fake urgency.
- Reaching under-16 users needs Roblox Kids/Select eligibility.

### Cited Findings

**Product types** [DOC] — [Monetization index](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/index.md); [Passes](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/passes.md); [Developer products](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/developer-products.md)
- Passes are a **one-time** fee for permanent privileges. Developer products can be bought repeatedly (currency, potions). Both cost 1 R$ to 1B R$.
- Passes can be promoted on Roblox's **Buy Robux page**, where they are given free to Robux-package buyers. This drives players to claim them in your game.
- `MarketplaceService:RankProductsAsync` returns a **personalized ordering** of products for your shop. It is rate-limited, so call it once at join.
- Cross-game developer product sales were disabled starting **2026-05-30**.

**Roblox's monetization guidance** [DOC] — [Monetization index](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/index.md); [Monetization foundations](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/game-design/monetization-foundations.md)
- "**limited time** … and **trading** … are particularly effective when combined."
- "A **weekly cadence** for updates is ideal. At the bare minimum, aim for monthly updates."
- Themed updates; "make users earn their access to new content".
- The shop should be integrated, contextual and inviting (a place to linger). Rotate stock.
- Season passes should have free and premium tracks, with reward spacing tuned to average session length.

**Subscriptions** [DOC] — [Subscriptions](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/subscriptions.md)
- Auto-renewing monthly. Up to 50 per game.
- Pricing: in Robux (≥49 R$, payout 70%, regional pricing forced on) or local currency ($2.99 / $4.99 / $7.99 / $9.99 / $14.99; payout 70% in month 1, then **100%**; requires ID or phone-verified creator; unavailable in some countries).
- Product types: Durable, Consumable or Currency.
- Rules:
  - Tiered mutually exclusive Bronze/Silver/Gold is not supported.
  - Don't gate paid benefits behind extra tasks.

- The Robux-subscription option was announced at RDC 2025. [SEARCH] — [DevForum: subscriptions in Robux](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/let-your-players-pay-for-subscriptions-within-experiences-in-robux/4552995?page=2)

**Roblox Plus** (launched **2026-04-30** at $4.99/month, replacing Premium for new buyers) [SEARCH] — [Bloxy News on X](https://x.com/Bloxy_News/status/2042646382855754150); [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/roblox/plus-subscription)

- Subscribers get **10% off** purchases, rising to **20%** from month 3. **Roblox subsidizes this, so the creator still earns 70 of a 100 R$ item.**
- Creators earn **250 R$/month for 3 months (up to 750 R$)** per new Plus subscriber acquired via `PromptRobloxSubscriptionPurchase`.
- Up to 100 R$ per subscriber who spends ≥60 min/month in your paid private servers.
- 10% of in-game Robux transfers by Plus subscribers.

**Creator Rewards** (replaced Premium engagement-based payouts on **2025-07-24**) [DOC] — [Engagement-based payouts (deprecated)](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/engagement-based-payouts.md); [Creator Rewards](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/creator-rewards.md)
- **Daily Engagement:** 5 R$/day per Active Spender with 10+ minutes, when your game is among their first 3 that day.
- **Audience Expansion:** **35% revenue share on the first $100** a **new or reactivated (60+ days lapsed)** user spends anywhere on Roblox in 60 days. The user must arrive via your Share Link, direct link or name search and play 10+ minutes, and your game must average **100+ DAU for 60 days**.

**Regional pricing / Managed Pricing**
- Launched for passes on **2025-04-22**. Later extended to developer products. **Paid random items policy** [DOC] — [Paid random items policy](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/paid-random-items.md)
- **Scope:** covers anything random bought with Robux **or with in-game currency purchasable with Robux**. Explicit examples:
  - "Hatch an egg for a pet", "Open a chest"
  - luck boosts, pity systems, rate-up items
  - combination items (fusing eggs)
- **Disclosure rules:**
  - Show every outcome's **percentage odds** before purchase, summing to 100%.

  - If an outcome can be obtained only once, update the remaining odds.
- **Exemption:** random rewards earned without Robux or Robux-bought currency (for example a found key) do not require odds.
- **PolicyService:**
  - If `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` is true, apply one of: a free earnable path, a fixed disclosed order, direct purchase priced at expected value, hiding the item, an error message, or teleporting the player away.
  - If `IsPaidItemTradingAllowed` is false, users may not trade outcomes of paid random items or other paid items.

- Simplest compliant path: sell boosts and passes, keep boxes Cash-only, or show odds everywhere from day one.

**Content rating**
- A "hood" setting with boxing should stay **Minimal/Mild**: cartoony knockouts, no realistic injury, no weapons, drugs or gang references, and no casino-like dice or slot props (those risk "unplayable gambling content", which means Moderate and loses ages 5–8).
- This keeps the full under-16 audience, which is the core simulator demographic.

**Free-to-play fairness**
- Keep paid perks as multipliers and convenience. Don't sell exclusive tactical power in any PvP crew battles.

### Gaps
- No official data on which specific simulator passes convert best. Price ladders above come from fan wikis (current at time of search) and are not tied to revenue outcomes.
- The exact date and enforcement deadline of the 2026 "Clarifying Requirements for Paid Random Items" DevForum post could not be read (DevForum blocked). The TechTimes article (2026-06-26) dates the worldwide change to about June 2026.
- Subscription adoption and conversion benchmarks are not public.
- No official guidance on how names or settings like "Hood" are treated by moderation or maturity labeling. Only the questionnaire criteria are documented.

## 5. Marketing and launch: ads, influencers, update days, events, communities, codes

### Takeaway
Launch traffic seeds the algorithm, but only organic RFY users count toward ranking. So ads and influencers buy you a test, and retention decides whether Roblox amplifies it.

Proven levers:
- Sponsored ads using the "Plays" objective, with up to 10 thumbnails tested
- **Share Links** with LaunchData perks, which also earn Creator Rewards Audience Expansion revenue
- **Experience Events** with opt-in notifications
- Saturday updates (Home traffic peaks Saturday) paired with a live admin event
- A personally visible developer on TikTok/YouTube (the Jandel model)
- Designing moments that make good 15-second clips

### Cited Findings

**Ads Manager** [DOC] — [Ads Manager](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/promotion/ads-manager.md)
- Available at 13+. Robux can be converted to ad credits (minimum 1 credit, irreversible).
- **Campaign goals:**
  - **Plays:** broad reach. Use it to test ideas or to provide "initial traffic to help recommendation systems learn what's working and qualify your game for Recommended For You".
  - **Earnings** (limited): reaches likely spenders.
  - **Engagement:** reaches age-checked highly engaged players toward the **Kids/Select 250-player threshold**, at a higher cost per play.
- Auto-bidding with "discounts designed for games that make Roblox overall healthier".
- **Up to 10 thumbnails** per campaign, including AI-generated variants. Ads run on Home and in search.
- Ads do not penalize RFY. However, an **ad-acquired user is excluded from RFY signals** and generally won't later see your game in RFY. [DOC] — [Discovery FAQ](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/discovery-faq.md)

**Ad cost anecdotes** (community, unverified)
- Small developers report cost per play from about 3 to 5.7 Robux. Advice is to spend 3k–10k Robux/day for 15–28 days, spread out rather than burst. [SEARCH] — [DevForum: optimizing CPP](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/optimizing-my-games-cost-per-play-from-ads/3047197); [DevForum: sponsorship costs and CCU](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsorship-costs-and-long-term-ccu-stability/3121727)

**Share Links and Creator Rewards** [DOC] — [Share links](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/promotion/share-links.md); [Creator Rewards](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/creator-rewards.md)
- Share links are trackable per channel and unlimited in number.
- Custom **LaunchData** can grant in-game perks to people joining via the link, for example an "influencer code" reward.
- New or returning users brought in this way earn the creator 35% of their first $100 platform spend, if the game holds 100+ DAU.

**Experience Events and Game Details page** [DOC] — [Discovery](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/discovery.md); [Experience events](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/promotion/experience-events.md)
- Players can opt in to be notified when an event starts. Events can show up to 5 thumbnails.
- The Game Details page and Roblox Groups are the "best way for creators to connect with and inform their communities".

**Update day**
- Home traffic peaks on **Saturday**. [DOC] — [Discovery](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/discovery.md)
- GaG and SaB both ran **Saturday or weekend admin-abuse events tied to update drops**. [SEARCH] — [GaG Fandom](https://growagarden.fandom.com/wiki/Admin_Abuse); [Wikipedia SaB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)

**Influencers and short-form video**

- Fan sites track codes for every hit (for example the Fortnite version of SaB). Codes are a community-media hook. [SEARCH] — [Beebom codes](https://beebom.com/fortnite-steal-the-brainrot-codes/)
- No primary data on code effectiveness was found.

### Inferences (applied to Hood)

**Launch sequence**
1. Soft launch and fix FTUE bounce, using funnel events and Experiments.
2. Get to Kids/Select eligibility: 250 highly engaged plays. Use a small Engagement or Plays ad budget, or pay the 1,000 R$ fee plus wait, or use a Plus subscription.
3. Run Plays ads with 5–10 thumbnails to find a winning creative, and copy that creative into organic thumbnail personalization.
4. Give influencers Share Links with a LaunchData perk (for example "a free Rare crew member"). These also accrue Audience Expansion rewards.
5. Lock a **Saturday update plus a "Block Party" admin event**, announced through Experience Events and the Group.

**Clip-ability**
- Design the wall-break moment to be a 10–15 second spectacle: slow-mo, a giant damage number, debris, crew cheering.
- A "punch power" number showing in the trillions is inherently shareable.

**Codes**
- Release one code per update and milestone (likes, group members) for free boosts. This gives creators and fans something to post.

### Gaps
- No verified cost-per-play benchmarks for 2026. The DevForum anecdotes are from 2024 and earlier and pre-date the 2026 ad changes.
- No primary data on the conversion impact of codes, group rewards or Discord size.

## 6. Practical "do this first" priorities for a small team building Hood

### Takeaway
In the 2026 Roblox environment the priority order is:
1. **Retention-first core loop and FTUE.** Beat the bounce buckets, earn D2–7 and D8–28 return days.
2. **Compliance plumbing.** PolicyService, odds UI, dynamic prices, Minimal/Mild content.
3. **Social visibility and co-play hooks.**
4. **A small, honest storefront.**
5. **A sustainable weekly cadence with a Saturday event ritual.**
6. **Only then paid acquisition and influencers.**

Monetization should be tuned after retention is strong, which is Roblox's own explicit advice.

### Cited Findings
- "If retention is low, focus on core gameplay first. Once retention is strong, improving monetization can help you increase your Home impressions." [DOC] — [Discovery FAQ](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/discovery-faq.md)
- The top RFY signals are PTR, first-play bounce (<60s and 61–180s), play days and playtime (capped at 60 min/day). The 28-day window has been live since 2026-06-15. [DOC] — [Discovery](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/discovery.md); [SEARCH] — [lensblox](https://lensblox.com/blog/roblox-algorithm-change-2026-explained/)
- Weekly updates are ideal and monthly is the minimum. Cadence releases should take under 3 weeks of effort. [DOC] — [Monetization index](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/index.md); [Content updates](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/game-design/content-updates.md)
- Configs and Experiments allow live tuning and A/B tests without restarts. [SEARCH] — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/roblox-announces-new-creator-tools-for-analytics-a-b-testing-and-live-ops-exclusive/)
- New games start 16+-only until 250 highly engaged plays in 60 days. [DOC] — [Kids and Select](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/publishing/kids-and-select.md)
- Paid random items need odds and PolicyService gating. [DOC] — [Paid random items](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/paid-random-items.md)
- Managed Pricing needs dynamic prices. [DOC] — [Managed pricing](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/managed-pricing.md)
- "Non-unique games" (clones) are deprioritized. [DOC] — [Discovery](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/discovery.md)
- Thumbnail personalization improved qPTR by +8.5% on average in Roblox's testing. [DOC] — [Thumbnails](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/publishing/thumbnails.md)
- Even the biggest 2025 hits decayed by 95–99% from peak within a year, and Roblox's own metrics show engagement moving to evergreen games. [SEARCH] — [Q2 2026 letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/Roblox-Q2-2026-Earnings-Shareholder-Letter.pdf); [bloxquiz GaG history](https://www.bloxquiz.gg/stats/grow-a-garden/history)

### Inferences: suggested build order for Hood (synthesized recommendations, not sourced facts)

**1. FTUE and the first five minutes (week 1)**
- Instant Power ticking. First training bag within 3 seconds.
- First wall break inside about 90 seconds, with big FX as the "joyful moment".
- First free crew box at about 3 minutes. First outfit equipped at about 4 minutes.
- Rebirth and next-neighborhood gate visible but locked.
- Instrument every step with funnel events.

**2. Return-day systems (week 1–2)**
- Offline Power/Cash accrual, capped and claimable on login.
- A 7-day login streak calendar with an escalating day-7 box.
- 3 daily quests and 1 weekly quest.
- A free playtime-gift ladder (5, 10, 20, 30 min) to beat the 10-minute Creator Rewards threshold and lengthen D1 sessions without exceeding the 60-minute cap.
- A Group-join reward.
- Experience notification opt-in ("your Power bank is full").

**3. Social visibility and co-play (week 2)**
- Crew members follow players.
- Overhead rebirth or neighborhood tag. Server leaderboard (Power, walls broken).
- Server-wide shout for rare box pulls and big wall breaks.
- Friend-in-server Power bonus. An optional "train together" duo bag.
- Later: crew trading, gated by `IsPaidItemTradingAllowed`.

**4. Progression math (week 2)**
- Geometric wall costs per world.
- Rebirth gives a predictable permanent multiplier.
- Each neighborhood is a new art set reusing existing systems, which makes cadence-friendly content.
- Keep all tuning numbers in **Configs** so they can be A/B tested.

**5. Compliance plumbing (before any paid items)**
- Use `PolicyService:GetPolicyInfoForPlayerAsync`.
- Odds "Details" panel on every box from day one, with luck boosts displayed numerically.
- No fake timers or fake sales. Neutral button copy ("View Item").
- Dynamic prices via `GetProductInfoAsync`.
- Answer the maturity questionnaire aiming for **Minimal/Mild** (cartoon boxing, no weapons, crime or gambling props).

**6. Storefront v1 (week 3)**
- Passes: 2x Power, 2x Cash, Auto-Train, VIP, +Crew Slots, Lucky Boxes.
- Developer products: timed boosts, a starter pack, Cash/box bundles (only if odds compliance is ready).
- Rewarded video for a 15-minute 2x boost.
- Enroll in Managed Pricing. Add a Plus subscription prompt (the creator earns up to 750 R$ per new subscriber).

**7. Discovery assets (launch)**
- 3–5 thumbnails, honest to gameplay, for personalization.
- A clean title with at most 1–2 emojis. A first-sentence description of the loop. An accurate gameplay video.

**8. Launch and growth (weeks 3–6)**
- Get through Kids/Select eligibility.
- Plays-objective ads with up to 10 creatives.
- Influencer Share Links with LaunchData perks.
- **Saturday update plus "Block Party" admin event** every week, announced via Experience Events.
- Seasonal or limited outfits combined with trading later. Roblox notes limited-time content plus trading are "particularly effective when combined".

**9. Measurement loop (ongoing)**
- Weekly review of Home Recommendations signals: PTR, bounce, D2–7 and D8–28 play days, co-play days.
- Retention cohorts per update. Run 1–2 Experiments per week (FTUE timing, reward sizes, price points).

- Kids/Select eligibility timelines in practice (how many days it typically takes to reach 250 highly engaged plays) are not documented publicly.
