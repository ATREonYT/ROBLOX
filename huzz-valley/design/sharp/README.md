# Huzz Valley sharp asset recreation

48 individually named transparent PNGs. Artwork was created with the built-in image generation tool from the supplied Huzz Valley UI V2 pages. The plain plus and menu controls are newly drawn geometric shapes rendered directly to PNG. These are new renderings, not enlarged source crops. Small icons are at least 256 px; larger artwork meets or exceeds four times the source slot dimensions. Original icon filenames are retained. For artwork without a standalone file, the kit's slot ID becomes the filename. See asset-map.csv for source mapping and verification.

## Missing references and interpretations

- The prior chat does not contain a complete, explicit list of the 15 small icons. This pack includes 15 icons covering all named functions and the main navigation/currency icons. The extra gear and star variants have been excluded.
- The source favourite icons are stars, while the requested list asks for hearts. No heart artwork was supplied. heart-filled and heart-outline are style-matched interpretations with new filenames.
- Seven crown colors/shapes are visible on pages/11-tier-index.png, but individual crown files do not exist in the ZIP. The requested names sub-1, sub-3, sub-5, mtb, htb, stacy, true-eve are used. Existing tier-mtb, tier-sub3, tier-sub5 and tier-stacy crops are text badges, not crowns, and are not replaced with crowns.
- Character, dance, cosmetic and reward references are small flattened page illustrations. Original high-resolution renders, 3D models and animation files are absent. Fine hair, face, clothing and effect details are reconstructed interpretations. These PNGs are static artwork, not rigs or animations.
- Locked character = card-hina; locked dance = dance-card-spin. Both reuse the corrected anonymous silhouette. The Sora bonus reuses the Sora card render for identity consistency. Their lock overlay is separate (icons/lock.png).
- Coin packs and reward currency have page artwork but no original standalone filename; descriptive names are assigned. Hearts likewise have new names.
- UI labels, favourite markers, ownership checks, frames, background scenes and preview pedestals are intentionally excluded from the artwork. The UI should render its own labels/overlays.
- Square transparent canvases preserve artwork proportions. Use aspect-preserving Fit when placing into nonsquare cards; do not stretch to fill.
