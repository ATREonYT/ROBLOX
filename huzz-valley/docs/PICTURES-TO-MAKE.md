# Pictures to make with your other AI

These are the pictures that are still stand-ins in the game: soft crops from your mockups, emoji, or our own
drawings. Make them in the same style as your mockups, then put them all in one zip and send it to me.

## The rules for every picture
- **Format:** PNG with a see-through (transparent) background.
- **Text:** none in the picture. The game writes the names and prices itself.
- **Size:** at least the size given below. Bigger is fine. Your Mac's screen is sharp, so small pictures look blurry.
- **File names:** exactly as written, in lower case.
- **Style:** the same as your Oct 17 mockups. That means bright anime Roblox, a soft glow and clean edges.

**Paste this at the start of each request to the other AI:**
> Make a game item picture for a kids' anime Roblox game, in the same style as the attached mockup. Transparent
> background PNG, no text, no frame or card behind it, the object centred with a little empty space around it,
> soft glow, bright saturated colours.
