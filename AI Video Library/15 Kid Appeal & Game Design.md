## What kids respond to (synthesis)
1. **Understand it in 10 seconds:** one big obvious action, and a thumbnail that shows it.
2. **Number go up, constantly:** big readable numbers, +1 popups and a progress bar that is always moving.
3. **First reward in under 60–90 seconds:** first upgrade, first wall break, first pet.
4. **Bright, saturated, toy-like:** saturated interactables on softer ground (see [[13 Cartoony & Low-Poly Building]]).
5. **Collecting and rarity:** pets, eggs, skins on a clear color rarity ladder (gray, green, blue, purple, gold, rainbow).
6. **Show off:** leaderboards facing spawn, auras, titles, rebirth badges.
7. **A reason to come back tomorrow:** daily rewards, playtime gifts, a weekly event, limited items.
8. **Juice on everything:** sound + shake + particles + bounce on each reward.
