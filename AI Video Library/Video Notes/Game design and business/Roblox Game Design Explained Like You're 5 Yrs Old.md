# Roblox Game Design Explained Like You're 5 Yrs Old

Back to [[00 Start Here]] · [[00 Video Notes Index]]

## TL;DR
Most hit simulator/tycoon-style games share the same underlying building blocks. To win, pick a reference game in your niche and change about 20% of it in the areas that matter most (fantasy and unique mechanic first). For 5 to 10K CCU that is enough; larger hits stack many innovations.

## Tools and setup
- No software. Games analysed: Ride a Pet, Steal an Egg, Escape Tsunami for Brainrots, Kick a Lucky Block, Dig and Clean, Surf and Rescue, Grow a Garden, Creatures of Sonaria, his own game "Raise Anomaly" (as heard "Raisin Anomaly").
- Framework: house analogy, foundation = fantasy + unique mechanic (the most differentiating parts).

## Step-by-step
1. [01:02] Chunk each game into building blocks: fantasy (the primary verb), unique mechanic, core loop, RNG, level design, tension, progression (speed/strength), collection.
2. [02:02] Core loop in all of them: "leave base, get thing" (return to base).
3. [02:02] RNG placement: post-acquisition (spawn table in world, roll happens once the thing is at base, e.g. Ride a Pet eggs, Steal an Egg) or pre-acquisition (roll is player-facing before you carry it, e.g. Kick a Lucky Block; Escape Tsunami just shows brainrots in the level).
4. [04:04] Tension: the "holy" anticipation moment (egg cracks in X seconds; monster; PvP; chaser).
5. [05:04] Progression: get faster/stronger; long-term collection: pets (or treasure in Dig and Clean).
6. [14:09] To start a new game: understand the genre, pick a reference game, change at least ~20% in meaningful areas.
7. [14:09] Compare the reference game against yours block by block (example: Steal an Egg vs Raise Anomaly) and innovate on fantasy and unique mechanic first.
8. [16:11] For bigger games, stack more changes (Ride a Pet changed about 40%: level design, tension, fantasy, unique mechanic).

## Exact settings and values
| Setting | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum change vs reference | 20% | enough for 5 to 10K CCU (also "1 to 5K, maybe 10K") |
| Ride a Pet change vs Steal an Egg | about 40% | his estimate |
| Egg/stock restock | every 5 minutes | Grow a Garden seeds and Steal an Egg eggs |
| Large hit target | 100K CCU | needs multiple stacked innovations |

## Prompts and skills used
No AI prompts. Table of examples:
- Ride a Pet: fantasy = ride a pet; unique = exploration (like Creatures of Sonaria with a simulator loop); post-acquisition roll; time pressure tension; innovative level design.
- Steal an Egg: fantasy steal a thing; unique = Grow a Garden style 5-minute stock refresh moved into the world; linear level design; PvPvE tension; get faster + collect pets.
- Escape Tsunami for Brainrots: fantasy escape with valuables; unique = "pressure valve" level design (run, decide whether to keep going, dip into a safe trough: tension then release); pre-acquisition; PvE; linear levels.
- Kick a Lucky Block: kick to unlock; unique = skill check (tap as bar fills); pre-acquisition; PvE; get stronger.
- Dig and Clean: dig up treasure; cleaning mechanic; post-acquisition roll; non-linear levels; get stronger; collect treasure.
- Surf and Rescue: save animals (likely different audience, maybe female); linear levels; PvE tension (shark, stingray).
- Raise Anomaly (his game): same as Steal an Egg but different audience/fantasy ("raise" thing) and a feeding mechanic.

## Gotchas and fixes
- Original ideas are rare; every game is derivative: innovate where it matters (fantasy, unique mechanic).
- Lack of tension (as in Dig and Clean) was a gap Surf and Rescue filled with a chaser.
- He leaves out progression pacing, first-five-minutes, retention and mutations, which also matter.

## Use it in our games
- Take a proven reference (e.g. a steal/brainrot or sim game) and change ~20%: new fantasy verb or audience plus one unique mechanic (as in the video; our application is inferred).
- Apply pressure-valve design: safe zones that release tension in steal-style maps (inferred).
- Use restock timers (every 5 minutes) for eggs/shop stock to drive return visits and urgency (inferred).
- Fill all blocks before building: fantasy, unique mechanic, core loop "leave base, get thing", RNG timing, tension, progression (+1 speed/strength) and collection (inferred).
