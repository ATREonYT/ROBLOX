# My Roblox Game Made $43,292 (Here's Exactly How to Monetize Yours)

Back to [[00 Start Here]] · [[00 Video Notes Index]]

## TL;DR
The Sell Potatoes tycoon earned $43,292 by favoring consumable developer products over one-time game passes, each solving a real player pain, placed in multiple purchase pathways, and iterated from watching real players.

## Tools and setup
- Roblox developer products (consumables) and game passes (one-time).
- Roblox sales data dashboard.
- Case game: Sell Potatoes, an idle tycoon (start with nothing, upgrade, automate, rebirth), similar to Sell Lemons.
- Road Developer Academy (paid, link in description) has full monetization setups.

## Step-by-step
1. [00:00] Avoid making everything a game pass: it sells once per player, so revenue depends on new players. Developer products are consumables, buyable repeatedly (toothpaste analogy).
2. [01:01] Top 3 products: speed boost (fixes slow movement; players who bought once re-bought next session), 5x cash (fixes grind; price not too cheap to wreck economy, not too expensive), auto collect (players didn't want to manually collect potato earnings).
3. [03:02] Find pain points by following a player around for 5 minutes and noting frustration, slowdowns, where fun stops. Those moments are product ideas.
4. [03:02] Purchase pathways: shop button in the HUD, a plus icon beside the cash counter, and developer product NPCs/buttons physically in the world where players spend time (they pass it 20 times per session).
5. [04:02] Game passes only for permanent changes: permanent 2x cash multiplier, VIP access, exclusive cosmetic. Make things like 2x offline earnings a developer product (rebought every login).
6. [05:02] Iteration loop: launch with basic monetization; watch players; check sales; add a product for a noticed pain; confirm it doesn't hurt session time and retention; keep or remove.

## Prompts and skills used
None stated in the video (no AI prompts).

## Gotchas and fixes
- Game passes feel bigger but only sell once per player.
- Cheap 5x cash can wreck the economy; too expensive means nobody buys it: find the middle ground.
- Products hidden in menus may never be seen; put them in the world and HUD.
- Check new products don't hurt session time and retention.

## Use it in our games
- Sell speed boost, 5x cash, and auto collect as repeatable developer products in a cartoony +1 simulator.
- Add a "+" icon beside the cash/stat counter and world-placed product NPCs near spawn and base (inferred for steal-a-brainrot style games).
- Keep permanent 2x cash and VIP as game passes; make 2x offline earnings a consumable.
- Playtest by watching someone for 5 minutes to find the next product (inferred).
