# Why 90% of Roblox Games Never Get Players

Back to [[00 Start Here]] · [[00 Video Notes Index]]

## TL;DR
A six-benchmark framework (onboarding, play time, retention, monetization, acquisition, first 14 days) run against the creator's game Steal a Superstar with real stats. Hitting enough of them gets a game picked up by the algorithm.

## Tools and setup
- Roblox Creator Dashboard analytics (retention, acquisition tab, monetization conversion).
- Paid ads: about $148 total spent on Steal a Superstar before home recommendations kicked in.
- Road Developer Academy (paid course, link in description) is promoted for the full monetization structure and ad strategy.
- Case game: Steal a Superstar: 35+ characters, 7 rarity tiers, base upgrades, rebirth; live about 2 months, peak 3,000 CCU, 1.4 million total visits.

## Step-by-step
1. [01:00] Benchmark 1, Onboarding: player understands the whole core loop in 2 to 5 minutes (what am I doing, why, what's next). Next goal should be reachable in another 10 to 15 minutes, no grind wall. In Steal a Superstar you spawn, see NPCs walking, steal one, it generates cash, loop clear within the first minute.
2. [02:00] Benchmark 2, Play time: average session above 10 minutes while running ads (before algorithm pickup); 20+ minutes average once home recommendations start.
3. [02:00] Run ads with a budget until it flops or home recommendations kick in.
4. [02:00] Benchmark 3, Retention: D1 12 to 15% ideal; D7 2 to 3% good; D30 has no universal target, higher means staying power.
5. [03:02] Benchmark 4, Monetization: create a problem and sell the solution. Build friction, sell what removes it.
6. [04:02] Benchmark 5, Acquisition tab: check friend joins and search clicks. Flat numbers mean no word of mouth, which caps algorithm push.
7. [05:04] Benchmark 6, first 7 to 14 days after publishing: be active in the game, watch players, fix broken things, adjust monetization. If the algorithm hasn't picked it up by day 14, reconsider continuing (exceptions exist).
8. [06:04] You don't need all benchmarks, just enough; most failures miss two or three without noticing.

## Prompts and skills used
None stated in the video (no AI prompts). Rebirth wipes money and progress but gives permanent multipliers, extra floors, extra slots, faster speed. "Nuke All" developer product kills every player on the server as fun social chaos.

## Gotchas and fixes
- Confusion after 5 minutes means broken onboarding, no mechanics fix that.
- Grind walls of an hour cause leaving.
- Retention numbers much higher or lower than 12%/2% would imply a very different game size.
- Flat friend joins/search clicks cap algorithm reach.
- Broken things go unnoticed by the dev but players find them: watch live in the first 14 days.

## Use it in our games
- Make the first +1 loop visible in under one minute and the first rebirth-style goal reachable in 10 to 15 minutes (inferred).
- Track D1 (12 to 15%), D7 (2 to 3%), and session length in the dashboard for each build.
- Use rebirth as intentional friction that sells multipliers, extra slots, and speed in steal games.
- Budget a small test ad spend (about $148 in this case) and watch for home recommendations.
- Add a fun chaos developer product like "Nuke All" (inferred) for a steal-a-brainrot style game.
