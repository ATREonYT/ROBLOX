# How I Make High Quality Roblox Thumbnails with AI

Back to [[00 Start Here]] · [[00 Video Notes Index]]

## TL;DR
Study top thumbnails, copy the principles (readable faces, one fantasy, teased mechanic, simple background, high contrast), test on mobile with qptr.io, then iterate with an AI image tool starting from a rough Canva mockup and new chats when it gets stuck. Test 2 to 3 new thumbnails per week.

## Tools and setup
- Canva (rough mockup from screenshots of your assets).
- AI image tool: "RoThumbs" (heard as "row thumbs"); says many free tools work too.
- qptr.io (free, by his friend Michael): preview thumbnails on mobile/desktop/console, by search, charts, icons. Not sponsored.
- creatorchange.io / Creator Exchange (heard as "creator exchange"): filter all games, sort by visits, search the verb keyword.
- Game examples studied: Grow a Garden 2, Hide from the Villain, Black Market Missile Empire, Paint and Seek, Car Flipper, Clean the Library, Hacker Business.

## Step-by-step
1. [00:00] Research top thumbnails and note patterns.
2. [00:00] Check each for: scene communicates the fantasy; emotions clearly visible; unique mechanic teased (often via text); implied challenge; core loop teased.
3. [05:04] Check on mobile with qptr.io: upload the thumbnail and view on mobile, desktop, console, search, charts, icons.
4. [06:05] Pick a concept to test: before/after (noob vs pro), first-person ("be a nurse/firefighter"), two-character scene (readable faces, simple background, color contrast), one character + clear action.
5. [11:07] Find themes: Creator Exchange > filter all games > sort visits > search the verb keyword (e.g. "hack") > open top game and note objects and themes (anonymous mask, green hacker stuff).
6. [12:09] Choose the most recognisable symbol for the fantasy (McDonald's golden arches for "hack a business").
7. [13:09] Make a loose Canva mockup from asset screenshots.
8. [13:09] Give mockup to the AI image tool with a specific prompt; iterate.
9. [15:09] Start a new chat when it keeps failing (use the base reference image only, restate the prompt).
10. [16:10] Simplify: shorter hills, bigger buildings, text not covering buildings, remove clouds for a clean sky and clear contrast, add a character (bacon/noob) if it helps.
11. [18:10] Recheck on qptr.io for all devices; ship, then keep the creative fresh.

## Prompts and skills used
- To recreate the concept: "I want to make a split-screen thumbnail. In both scenes, there will be a business on a hill in the background. Make it big, though. The scene on the left will be McDonald's on the hill. There were purple files, see reference image, flowing from the McDonald's down to the bottom of the hill, where there were only a few servers and antennas placed. The scene on the right is exactly the same as the scene on the left, except it's the Apple Store, the computer business, not the fruit. And there are a ton more advanced servers and antennas at the bottom of the hill."
- "Make this look like an actual Roblox thumbnail. On the left, it should say level one. On the right, it should say level 999."
- Follow-ups: make the hill shorter, buildings larger, text must not cover the buildings, "make the businesses really big with the logos very big", "remove the clouds. Clear color contrast", "make that logo huge, easily readable".
- Clear blue sky and green grass, purple files popping on green.

## Gotchas and fixes
- Looks good on desktop but unreadable on mobile: always check on qptr.io.
- Logo unreadable on mobile: zoom in on logos, enlarge.
- AI "Apple" came as a fruit: specify "the Apple Store, the computer business, not the fruit".
- Too visually busy (brown, clouds): shorten hill, remove clouds.
- Text kept covering the business: restart in a new chat with the main prompt.
- Characters facing the wrong way: new chat with reference image.
- QPTR always declines with more impressions: test fresh thumbnails regularly.

## Use it in our games
- Make thumbnails with 1 to 2 readable-emotion characters, tease the unique mechanic in a few big words, simple bright background (inferred).
- Use before/after or "Level 1 vs Level 999" concept for +1 progression sims (inferred).
- Test every thumbnail on qptr.io mobile view before upload (inferred).
- Plan a weekly rotation of 2 to 3 new thumbnails per game (as in the video) while watching click-through (inferred).
