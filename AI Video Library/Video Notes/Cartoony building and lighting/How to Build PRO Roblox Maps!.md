# How to Build PRO Roblox Maps!

Back to [[00 Start Here]] · [[00 Video Notes Index]]

## TL;DR
A one-hour timelapse of building a clicker/simulator game map: 250x250 grass base, fixed lighting, spawn at a corner or edge, platforms for points of interest, curved paths, border wall, then filler (trees, rocks, grass, terrain, waterfall) and finally placing the game features near spawn. The layout is designed to guide players outward from spawn.

## Tools and setup
- Roblox Studio (default Baseplate-style part for ground).
- Archimedes plugin (smooth curves for paths and circular structures).
- Premade assets from earlier projects (trees, rocks, grass models, platforms, border parts, terrain parts, portals, shop, eggs, leaderboards, wheel spin).
- Creator offers lighting settings and the finished map via download in the description (not available in the transcript).

## Step-by-step
1. [00:00] Scale one part to about 250x250 for the grass ground.
2. [00:00] Fix the default Roblox lighting first (creator uses his own saved settings, values not stated).
3. [00:00] Pick the spawn location and the points of interest. Spawn goes in a corner or near a side so players are guided across the map as they progress.
4. [01:00] Replace placeholder points of interest with nicer platforms; recolor the spawn part to the beige platform color.
5. [01:00] Replace the circle spawn with a more unique shape: take a small part and use Archimedes to make smooth curves. Add a darker section for variety.
6. [01:00] Make paths from spawn with Archimedes. Tip: never change the angle you adjust the parts at, for a cleaner consistent style.
7. [02:00] Paths layout: a big circle around the map, plus inner paths linking to points of interest. Add a grander pathway leading to the portals (main route for experienced players).
8. [02:00] Add border: build a row of about five border parts, copy and paste around the whole map as a big wall.
9. [02:00] Add details: large trees along the borders first, then large rocks (big filler first so you do not overdo it and can fit smaller pieces around them), then grass models, then smaller trees inside.
10. [03:01] Fill big open green areas with terrain parts; place rocks along their bottoms as a transition.
11. [03:01] Add a waterfall for extra colors beyond brown and green; place rocks along the border part it comes out of.
12. [03:01] Select all border parts and duplicate them (done on every map; makes it look more complete).
13. [04:02] Recolor some border parts around the waterfall to reduce the amount of green.
14. [04:02] Place game features: leaderboards near spawn, wheel spin near spawn (free way to progress), shop and starter eggs near spawn, Titanic pet at spawn (to push Robux sales).
15. [04:02] Place all portals, add a second platform to fit five portals, put the VIP portal in its own separate area.

## Gotchas and fixes
- Default lighting looks bad: change it before building.
- Empty map confuses players: add platforms and details so it reads clearly.
- Big open green spaces: fill with terrain parts and rock transitions.
- Too much green: recolor some border parts near the waterfall.
- Place big filler first, small filler later, to avoid overdoing it.

## Use it in our games
- Put spawn in a corner or edge and use a clear circular path plus a grand main path to the portals/zones so new players are pulled outward.
- Cluster shop, eggs, wheel spin, leaderboard and the top monetized pet near spawn; put VIP in its own area.
- Use Archimedes-style smooth curved paths and a fixed path angle for a consistent cartoony look (inferred for our simulator maps).
- Build in order: layout, border wall, big filler, small filler, transitions, color accents (waterfall) to avoid monotone green (inferred for steal-style bases).
