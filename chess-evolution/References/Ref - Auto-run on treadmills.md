# Ref: Auto-run on treadmills

![[frames/auto-run-treadmill.jpg]]

**What it shows.** The avatar stands on a purple treadmill (the "x5"-style tier with pink crown posts) and **runs by itself**. Auto Wins, Auto Train Here and the FREE Auto Clicker all read OFF, and the mouse is still. Gains keep coming anyway: "+2.1K" pop-ups about 4 times a second, and the Level bar goes 83.2K → 142.3K in about 7 s (≈ 8.4K a second). The Subscribers counter ticks 2.8M → 2.9M.

**Details worth copying**
- Standing on the deck is enough to train. The avatar plays a full running cycle in place, centred on the belt, facing the console.
- The belt surface is a bright purple with a sparkly, starry texture, and the frame has a glowing rim. The treadmill reads as "active" without extra particles.
- The pop-ups are the same style as [[Ref - Subscribers pop-up on tap]], spawned at random spots around the running avatar.
- The camera is free; the player orbits around the machine while it runs.
- Floating world labels above other machines ("x99 Subscribers", "x999 Subscribers", "UNLOCKED") are big outlined text, readable from across the room.
- A rebirth-style big counter ("249" with a coin icon, top left) appears briefly when it changes.

**For Chess Evolution:** passive training on the deck at a fixed tick (about 4 a second here), on top of tapping. Check this against the master prompt and the Auto Train pass before building, because our current loop is tap-only.
