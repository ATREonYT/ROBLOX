ROBLOX MAP TEXTURE PACK

38 seamless 512 × 512 RGB color tiles
7 matching tangent-space normal maps for the studded materials
Texture_Preview.png shows the full palette.
Material_Map.json maps the color textures to their optional normal maps,
base colors, roughness values, and starting repeat sizes.

The tiles and colors were recreated from the visible map reference.
They are approximations of how the source surfaces look in the screenshots.
The gate panels' transparency is a material setting. The gate PNGs are
opaque color swatches. The pack covers environment surfaces and display
bases for a map-only rebuild.

MATCH THE LEFT MAP

Hub lawn and lane banks
  Grass_Lime on the main lawn and bank tops. Use Grass_Bright in selected
  sunlit grass patches and Grass_Cap on narrow green rim pieces.

Orange path and trench walls
  Path_Orange for the walking surface and Path_Edge_Brown for the separate
  narrow border geometry. Use Dirt_Wall_Orange on exposed vertical walls.
  Dirt_Wall_Shade works on recessed sides. Give wall faces their own UVs.

Display stands and lane paving
  Paving_Grey for the main platform, Paving_Light for the shelf tops and
  exposed trim, Paving_Bay_Grey for darker bay floors, and Paving_Riser for
  risers and sloped transitions. Use Paving_Dark_Trim on thin edging.
  Display stand lower pads go mint, cyan, red, black, black. Use
  Display_Black on the six upper plinths.

Vegetation and water
  Leaves_Lime for primary canopies, Leaves_Sunny for brighter tops,
  Leaves_Green for shaded conifer tiers, and Shrub_Lime for low shrubs.
  Use Bark_Orange and Bark_Pale for the two trunk colors. Use Water_Cyan
  on the flat water surface and Stone_Shore on the gray shoreline blocks.

Stage lane and portal
  Paving_Grey under the bays. Use Stage_Pad_Red on the large rectangles,
  Stage_Marker on the small dark markers, Stage_Tile_Magenta and
  Stage_Tile_Yellow on the side tiles, and Stage_Divider_Lime on the
  thin cross strips. Gate colors work as frame tints. Apply the matching
  tint with roughly 84% transparency as a starting point for a separate
  gate panel. Use Portal_Blue for the shell and Portal_Magenta for its core.

BLENDER

In Blender 5.1, choose File > Append, open Map_Materials.blend, and
select the materials you need from the Material folder. The PNGs are
packed inside the file. Assign the appended materials to your meshes.

As an alternative, in Blender 4.x or later, open Import_Materials_Blender.py as an external
file in the Scripting workspace's Text Editor and choose Run Script.
The script adds the Map_ materials to the current file and packs the
images. Assign materials to the matching meshes. It does not auto-assign
materials to objects. Each studded tile contains 8 × 8 studs. Scale the
UVs to repeat once per 8 world studs in each direction as a starting
point. A 32 × 16-stud face would use four by two UV repeats. The water
texture starts at one repeat per 24 studs.

For manual setup, connect the color PNG to Base Color using sRGB.
Set the normal PNG to Non-Color and connect it through a Normal Map
node. Start with strength 0.32. Use repeat wrapping and keep the UV
spacing consistent across adjoining pieces.

ROBLOX STUDIO

Upload the required Color PNGs as image assets. For Parts, apply a
repeating Texture to the corresponding face. Start at StudsPerTileU
and StudsPerTileV of 8 for studded surfaces. Use separate textures
on the top and the exposed walls. For UV-mapped meshes, carry over
the same UV repeats and use the color PNG as the color texture.
The color PNGs include shallow shading on the studs, so they still
read as raised when used on their own.
