# What to design for World 1 (for Claude Design or any image/3D AI)

These are the objects that make the game look "front-page". I build the map, code, effects and UI around them. The objects themselves look much better when you design them.

Already done by you: the 5 boxes (Street, Graffiti, Chrome, Crown, Grail Bundle) and the 22 shoes. In progress: the 10 guns.

## How to send each object (same as your box and shoe sheets)
- **One object per image**, on a plain light background.
- **Views:** ¾ view and side view, plus front view for signs and boards.
- **Style:** the soldier game's toy style: chunky, rounded edges, glossy, 3-5 bright colours, clean silhouette. No studs, no tiny details (nothing smaller than a finger on a real toy).
- **File name = the name in the first column** (e.g. `Pad_VIP.png`). If the tool can export 3D (.glb / .fbx / .obj), send that too; it's even better.
- The size column says how big it stands in the game (1 stud ≈ a Roblox brick; the player is ~5 studs tall).
