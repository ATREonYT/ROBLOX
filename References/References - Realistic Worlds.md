# References - Realistic Worlds

Back to [[Reference Gallery]] · [[00 Build Router]] · How to read and use them: [[33 Realistic World Building Playbook]] §1

Alex shared these on 2026-11-01 as the look to aim for in realistic RP maps ("I want this project to be the best game of RP on Roblox"). They come from other creators' games: **learn from them, never copy** buildings, signs, names or textures. Files: `Build Playbooks/references/realistic-worlds/` (resized to 1600 px JPG).

## Sunny West Coast hood
Patched and stained asphalt, mottled concrete, graffiti at reachable heights, palms and power lines over flat roofs, painted kerbs, a yellow hatch box, rusty hydrant, floating world-space job icons.

## Humid coastal town
Dense three-height foliage, tall leaning palms, heavy haze, clean curving roads with double yellow lines and jersey barriers, believable building types, shallow turquoise water with breakwaters, markers, a wreck, a pier and a bridge.
