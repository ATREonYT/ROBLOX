# North Miami RP: screenshot breakdown (Nov 3, 2026)

Taken live in Studio: shots 1 to 8 during a real Play session (real HUD, intro, tutorial), shots 9 and 10 in Edit mode from the Quality Lab. Nothing in the game was changed. The Edit-mode clock was put back to 21:00 afterwards.

## 1. Supermarket interior
**Good:** The fridge wall is the best interior in the game so far. Lit glass doors, lots of varied bottles, ceiling light panels and a security dome all read as a real store.
**Cheap:** The walls and ceiling are flat white with nothing on them, so the room looks unfinished. The checkout counter is plain red and yellow blocks. The phone peeks up at the bottom right and covers part of the scene even when it's closed.
**Fix next:** Add a tile or texture to the floor and walls, put price signs and posters up, and give the counter a real register model.

## 2. Intro cutscene, Liberty Food Mart
**Good:** This looks like a real game. Letterbox bars, the "YOU (THINKING)" dialogue box, the typewriter text, the power lines, the realistic Ford sedans and the big red sign all sell the opening.
**Cheap:** The parking lot is one flat dark brown colour with no cracks, oil stains or texture. The building's top half is a long blank grey wall.
**Fix next:** Asphalt texture on the lot, and a roof line or rooftop AC units to break up the wall.

## 3. Intro cutscene, Liberty Blvd and Flagler St
**Good:** The strongest shot of the set. Traffic lights on mast arms, green street signs, the brick apartment with the "RISE" graffiti, palm trees and the yard sale give it a real Miami street feel.
**Cheap:** The avatar is a default blocky character, and the "No job." line sits on top of it. The crosswalk is crisp, but the sidewalk curb is plain grey blocks.

## 4. HUD in daylight
**Good:** The HUD is clean and readable. The cash pill with the money icon, the level and XP, the health heart, the objective pill ("Head inside Liberty Food Mart"), the round minimap with a route, the fist hotbar slot and the "[M] Phone" hint all look professional, and nothing overlaps.
**Cheap:** The minimap shows grey blocks without street names. The "310 m" distance tag on the minimap and the floating park marker repeat information.

## 5. Aerial view of the neighbourhood
**Good:** The block layout reads well from above: corner stores, small houses, a hatched intersection, a railway with hazard stripes.
**Cheap:** This is the weakest view. Roofs are flat black with nothing on them, the ground between buildings is bare, the roads are pure black with no texture, and there are hardly any trees. Players will see this every time they drive past at height.
**Fix next:** Roof clutter (AC units, vents, satellite dishes), grass and dirt patches, more palms, and a road texture.

## 6. Sunset street
**Good:** Lovely. The sun bloom behind the house, the pink clouds and the warm light on the mart make this the best "trailer" shot. The green Lamborghini Urus at the kerb looks great.
**Cheap:** The road is still pure black, so it looks like a hole in the ground next to the warm buildings.

## 7. Night street
**Good:** The stars and the glowing mart windows look nice, and the red traffic lights still glow.
**Cheap:** The whole scene goes purple and flat. The street lamps on the poles don't seem to be lit, so there are no pools of light on the road and the crosswalk glows lavender. This is the biggest visual problem I saw.
**Fix next:** Turn on real lights in the street lamps (SpotLight pointing down), and make the night ambient colour more blue and darker so the lights stand out.

## 8. Phone open
**Good:** The phone looks like a real modern iPhone: dynamic island, date and big clock, rounded app icons (Jobs, Settings, Money, Garage) and a dock with Messages (with a red "2" badge), Calls, Maps and Camera.
**Cheap:** The home screen is half empty, with only four apps and a big blank gap. The phone clock said 9:00 PM while the sky had only just turned dark, so the phone time and the game time look slightly out of sync.
**Fix next:** Fill the grid with more apps (even "coming soon" ones like Bank, Contacts, Social) or add a wallpaper.

## 9. Job tools on test dummies
**Good:** The tools are in the hands at the right grip: scanner, spray bottle, sponge.
**Cheap:** Next to the hands the tools look tiny, and the spray bottle is plain white with no label. The dummies show it's still a test bench.

## 10. Robbery gear: masks, duffels, props
**Good:** The ski masks and the black duffel bags look right for robberies. The pigeons are a fun, realistic ambient detail.
**Cheap:** The boxes next to the duffels are flat coloured cubes. One pigeon's wings are spread wide and frozen in mid-flight while it sits on the floor.

## Not captured
- **Getaway car:** I didn't find a model with that name. The cars in the world are the Ford sedans and the two Urus models.
- **New flat/apartment:** `MTL_FlatPreview` is in the place, but from the camera angles I tried it only showed a plain box and a wall, so I left it out instead of sending a bad picture.
- Studio dropped the connection twice and Play stopped partway through, so I took the last two shots in Edit mode.

## Top 5 upgrades, in order of impact
1. Lit street lamps and a less purple night.
2. Asphalt texture on roads and parking lots (pure black looks cheap in every outdoor shot).
3. Rooftop clutter, plus grass and trees between buildings for the aerial view.
4. Detail on the plain walls inside and outside (texture, posters, signs).
5. Fill the phone home screen.
