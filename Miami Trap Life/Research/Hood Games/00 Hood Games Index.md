# Hood games knowledge base

This folder is the start of the knowledge base on Roblox hood and street RP games: how they play, how they make money, how their screens look, and what keeps players coming back. Add to it whenever you study a game. Keep one file per subject, and mark where every number came from.

## In this folder
| Note | What's in it |
|---|---|
| [[Game Profiles]] | Each game (Da Hood, South Bronx, Tha Bronx 3, Philly Streetz 2, Hood Rivals, Streetz War 2 and more): numbers, loop, what to learn |
| [[Economy, Jobs and Crime]] | The money ladder, dirty money and washing it, jobs, crime loops, police, survival stats, the dream item |
| [[Cars, Drip, Housing and Combat]] | Car ladder and garage, clothes and masks, apartments and safes, knock-then-finish combat |
| [[Monetization, Social and Retention]] | What the genre sells, crews and trading, live ops and codes, why players stay and why they leave |
| `GUI/` | Screenshots from each game (one folder per game, with `notes.md` and a `_sheet.jpg` contact sheet) |
| [[GUI Patterns - Breakdown]] | The patterns across all the GUIs: layout, money, stats, hotbar, phone, shops, prompts, fonts and colours |
| [[GUI Redesign Spec]] | Our own GUI redesign, built from the breakdown and the vault's earlier UI lessons |

## Rules for this folder
- **Study, don't copy.** We learn layouts and patterns. We never copy another game's art, icons, logo, names or exact screens.
- **Say where it came from:** *(seen)* in a frame, *(wiki)* from a guide, or general knowledge to check.
- **Kid-safe wording** in anything that ends up in the game (see the game's `CLAUDE.md`).
