# Hood game profiles

What the table says:
- **Da Hood is the old giant.** 3 billion visits, but players now are a few percent of its peak and its like ratio is the lowest here. It still sells 132 developer products, which tells you how much of its money comes from small repeat buys (cash, ammo, skins).
- **New hood games peak fast and fall fast.** Tha Bronx 3 and Hood Rivals both launched in August 2026 and are already well below their peaks. A launch spike needs a weekly update rhythm behind it or it fades in about six weeks.
- **High like ratios (90%+) come from fair, focused games.** Tha Bronx 3 (92%) and Hood Rivals (90%) both rate far above Da Hood (70.5%), which players mostly complain about for cheaters and pay-to-win.

## Da Hood (2019, the genre's template)
- **What it is.** A free-roam PvP sandbox in a small blocky city. No jobs to speak of: money (DHC) comes from robbing ATMs and registers, killing, and buying it with Robux.
- **Loop.** Spawn, buy a gun at one of the shops on the map, fight, rob, buy armor and better guns, lose it all, repeat.
- **Signature mechanics.** **Knock then stomp**: players go down first and someone has to finish them, which turns every fight into a little drama with a rescue window. Carrying and grabbing downed players. Crews (group-based teams with a tag over your head). Masks hide your name. A gym and food that change your body. Police team with cuffs and jail.
- **Money.** Dozens of Robux-for-DHC products, gun skins, and passes. The 132 developer products are the clearest sign of a "buy small, often" economy.
- **Why people stayed.** Skill PvP with a high ceiling (aim, movement, "camlock" culture), crews and rivalries, YouTube montage culture, and it was first.
- **Why it fell.** Cheaters (it is one of the most-scripted games on the platform), pay-to-win cash, and very little to do if you don't want to fight.
- **Learn from it.** Knock then finish is the genre's best combat idea and we already have it (GunDowned). **Don't** sell cash so cheaply that it breaks the economy, and plan anti-exploit work from day one.
- **Spin-offs.** Hood Customs, Hood Modded, Da Hood Aim Trainer and many copies took Da Hood's map and combat and stripped out the grind for pure PvP. They show that a lot of players come only for the fighting.

## South Bronx: The Trenches (2022, the roleplay template)
- **What it is.** A slower "realistic" roleplay city on the Southside of the Bronx. Citizens, groups (gangs and companies), jobs, buying, selling and trading. The description bans random killing and fail-RP.
- **Signature mechanics.** *(wiki)* Jobs at stores that are "hiring", holding more than one job, sending money to other players **through the phone** (pick a receiver, type an amount), buying cars, clothes, food and weapons with Cash. Character creation picks hair, face and an **RP name** that stays with you.
- **Platforms.** PC, mobile and PlayStation, so the UI has to work with a controller.
- **Why people stay.** The roleplay itself: groups, a name, and the slow feeling of "making it". The phone is the centre of social life.
- **Learn from it.** A phone that moves money between players is a social feature, not just a menu. An RP name and a group tag give people an identity worth defending.

## Tha Bronx 3 (Aug 2026, the current hit)
- **What it is.** The third game in the "Tha Bronx" line: a New York crime grind with survival stats.
- **Stats.** *(wiki)* Hunger and energy bars that affect sprinting and fighting. You fix energy by sleeping, coffee or lemonade. You fix hunger by cooking cheap raw meat on a BBQ grill ($10), which the wiki recommends over buying fast food. Skill points that you spend in the menu (codes also give skill points).
- **Money ladder.** *(wiki)* Start with $1,000.
  - Mop job at the burger place: about $45 an item, "skip it".
  - Construction: about $1,200 a block at level 12, the safe steady earner.
  - Trap house: kick in doors, loot dirty money, best on empty servers.
  - Studio robbery: three tiers, $3,000 / $7,500 / $16,000, needs a gun, has cooldowns, NYPD risk.
  - Card scams: fake cards, ATMs and a laptop.
  - Bank robbery: C4, duffel bag and ski mask, the highest ceiling, needs a crew.
  - House robbery: buy access to other players' houses and steal TVs and safes.
- **Dirty and clean money.** Crime pays dirty money. You wash it at the laundromat before banking it or buying top-tier gear. Some shops (the gun store, the black market by the bank) take dirty cash.
- **Gear ladder.** Drake backpack ($700) to carry more, Glock at the gun store, ski mask at the "Drip Store", C4 at the black market, and the "Switch" (full-auto kit) at EXO Guns as the end-game item, also sold as a Robux pass and traded on a player black market.
- **Controls.** *(wiki)* Shift to sprint (stamina), E to interact (hold E for drilling and bagging), number keys or scroll for weapons, R to reload, right click to aim. The menu holds settings, codes and skill points. The inventory equips bags, masks, food and C4 by click or drag.
- **Updates.** An "overhaul" re-release brought a new UI, new job pay and more gun stores. Seasonal events (winter, Halloween, February) each came with a code.
- **Learn from it.** This is the clearest version of the loop in [[What Makes Hood RP Games Fun]]: a ladder of money methods from safe and slow to risky and fast, dirty money that has to be washed, and one dream item (the Switch) everyone talks about.

## Philly Streetz 2
- **What it is.** "Next-generation hood roleplay" set in Philadelphia, by JalenBoston and On1yDJ. *(guide)* Legit jobs or street hustles, guns, lootable spots, credit-card scams, a reputation, and customisable cribs (apartment interiors).
- **Learn from it.** Customisable apartments are a status item that other players can visit. That fits our flat and power-bill system.

## Hood Rivals (Aug 2026)
- **What it is.** A deathmatch shooter in a hood setting (RoTrends files it under "Deathmatch Shooter"). 57M visits in three months, 90% likes, only 5 game passes.
- **Learn from it.** Fair, focused PvP with few passes still rates very well. A separate "fight mode" or arena could serve players who only want to shoot without ruining RP for everyone else.

## Streetz War 2 (London, 2022)
- **What it is.** *(guide)* A London-set roleplay city: pick gender, look and a name, then a **guided tour** points out stores (Zara- and JD-style), job sites, the bank, apartments and the police station.
- **Jobs.** Moving boxes, mopping, baking pizzas.
- **Money safety.** ATMs all over the city. Money in the bank is safe when you die; cash on you is not.
- **After a death** a player ID shows on screen so you can report unfair kills on Discord.
- **Learn from it.** The guided tour on first join, and "banked money is safe, pocket money is not", which creates a reason to walk to an ATM.

## Others worth a look
- **1700 Chicago, Out West Chicago 2, No Safety 2:** "realistic GTA RP" games: real-looking guns, full-body ragdolls, cops, Chicago neighbourhoods. Covered in [[What Makes Hood RP Games Fun]] §1.
- **Vice City Miami 2:** the closest to our setting. Its money guide is summarised in [[What Makes Hood RP Games Fun]].
