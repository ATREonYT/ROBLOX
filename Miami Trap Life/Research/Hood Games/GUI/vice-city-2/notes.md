## The overall look
- **Style:** dark, sleek and techy, with **angled/chamfered corners** and thin white bracket lines. It feels like a GTA-V/FiveM HUD. The font is **Montserrat/Gotham bold** for numbers and a lighter weight for labels.
- **Money, top-right:** two stacked rows. Each has a small grey label ("Cash", "Bank") above a bold white number ("$56,015", "$0"). To the right of each is a **hexagon badge**: Cash is a glossy green hexagon (~RGB 30,200,80, with an outer green glow) holding a white banknote glyph, and Bank is a dark-grey hexagon holding a white bank/pillars glyph. A thin white vertical bracket line with dot ends runs down the right of both (see `hud-cash-bank-closeup`).
- **Keybind hints, right-middle:** a column of "Sprint [LShift]", "Emote [V]", "Phone [X]" (plus "Drive W", "Break/Reverse S", "Lights L" in a car). White bold 10 px label, then a small dark rounded key-cap with a thin white border. A bracket line connects them, as with the money.
- **Energy, bottom-left:** a round dark-blue badge with a white lightning-bolt icon, "ENERGY" in 7 px white caps, and a **cyan bar** (~RGB 40,190,230) under it.
- **Hotbar, bottom-centre:** small squares (~2.6% wide) with a dark translucent fill and white captions ("Fists", "Phone", "Car key", "Drill", "Television"). The selected slot has a **white 2 px outline**. A red notification dot with a number appears on the Phone slot.
- **Overhead name tags:** RP name in white semi-bold (~"Tyrone Hall"), Roblox username under it in **green** (~RGB 60,200,90, "MoeBandz_TOD"). Armour shows as a **white shield icon** on the character's back.
- **Action toasts, bottom-centre above hotbar:** a dark translucent rounded card. It has a green circle icon (bell/check), a category in **green caps** ("HAM PALACE", "JOB", "ROBBERY", "DELIVERY"), a white message ("Added Onions", "Grabbed Television."), and a thin **green progress line** at the bottom.

**Takeaways for Miami Trap Life:** Vice City 2 already owns the "iPhone with a red wave wallpaper + parody apps" look and the replica-printing hustle. Its HUD language is chamfered dark panels, white bracket lines, hex money badges, green-caps toasts with progress lines, and a dripping-red "DEAD". To stand out, MTL should not copy the hex + bracket look. A different shape language (e.g. pills or neon Miami pastel) would differentiate it.
