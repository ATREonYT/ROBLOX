## Patterns across the games (what is "normal" for a hood RP GUI)
- **Money is always the loudest thing**, and it is almost always **green** (Da Hood, South London, Philly, Streetz War, Vice City). Games with two balances show **cash + bank/dirty money** side by side, and the second is coloured differently (red in TB3, pink in Philly, grey hex in Vice City).
- **Vital stats sit bottom-left or above the hotbar.** Pill bars with a small icon (TB3, Philly, Da Hood) beat rings (South London) for readability. Hunger, energy/sleep and stamina are standard in RP games. PvP games show only health.
- **The hotbar is always bottom-centre**, small squares, and **the selected slot gets a blue outline** in 8 of the 10 games (Vice City 2 uses white; OFF1700 uses a cyan-white outline in the inventory). Players expect it. Names are often text-only in the slot.
- **Interaction = "[E] + two lines" pill** (small grey object name over a white action) or a hold-ring. Used by Streetz War 2, Outwest, Philly and Vice City 2.
- **The phone is an iPhone clone** (Vice City 2, Streetz War 2, South London). Its apps are Maps, Messages/Twitter, a supplier app (REPZ), a shopping app (RoMazon) and a buyer-chat for drug runs. Twitter-style posts double as world announcements in two games.
- **Shops are list menus, not image grids**, in the RP games: name, stock, price and one green Buy (TB3, Philly, South Bronx, Vice City). Image cards appear only in Robux/cosmetic shops (South London, Da Hood skins, Hood Customs).
- **Death screens:** a red-tinted world plus "killed by NAME" plus a respawn countdown. Flavour text adds identity ("GUESS YOU AIN'T BUILT FOR THESE STREETS YOUNG BOL", "YOU WERE MURKED BY", dripping "DEAD"). Extras: Respawn vs paid "Keep Inventory" (Outwest), a downed "*Critical Condition*" tag (South London).
- **Status banners** for safe zone / immunity / combat-logged / camera-seen / rent timer / house arrest are short text lines or small chips, not big modals.
- **Keybind hint lists** (bottom-left or right-middle) are now common in 2025-26 games (Vice City 2, Da Hood 2026, South London, South Bronx controller list).
- **Robbery and job feedback:** green "+$" pop-ups, toasts with a category in caps plus a progress line (Vice City 2), counters like "0/20" (Outwest bank) and "Attempts: 1" (TB3 car theft).
- **Fonts:** Gotham/Montserrat bold dominate. Heavy italic display fonts appear in the simulator-style games (South London). Serif and monospace appear only in older or FiveM-style ones (TB3, OFF1700).

## What this means for Miami Trap Life
- Vice City 2 is the direct Miami competitor and already uses chamfered panels, hex badges, a red-wave iPhone and REPZ printing. **Do not copy its shape language.** The current MTL direction from [[UI Art Direction - Lessons]] (dark grey 24,25,29 panels, white text, green only on money, soft blue selection, round hotbar slots, eased motion) is already distinct from it.
- Copy the conventions players expect: green money top-right, a blue selected slot, an [E] two-line prompt, a cash/bank split, and short status chips.
- The best single reference for a clean but rich HUD is **Philly Streetz 2** (round icons + pill bars + money). The best for systems is **Tha Bronx 3** (needs bars, injury silhouette, rent and camera warnings) and **Outwest Chicago 2** (loot transfer, Keep Inventory).
