## The overall look
- **Minimal, "AAA/FiveM" style.** There is no permanent money box. The top-right has only "Cash:" label left and the number far right ("Cash:        2725"), both white regular sans with no background. A thin **green health pill** (~RGB 60,220,120, rounded ends) appears above the cash when damaged.
- **Top-left:** tiny dark-pill buttons ("Buy Cash", plus icon buttons) and a "VIEW WANTED STATS" pill in 7 px white caps on dark grey.
- **Hotbar, bottom-centre:** square slots (~3.5% wide) in **dark translucent grey** (~RGB 40,40,40, 60%), no border. They have slot numbers top-left, item names as small white text ("Phone", "Routes Drill", "Duffel Bag", "PD Rifle | Police Gear"). The selected slot has a **2 px blue (~RGB 80,140,255) outline**.
- **Fonts:** clean geometric sans (Gotham/Montserrat-like) for menus. A **monospace (Courier-like)** font is used for system warnings (the combat-log box).
- **Top-centre spawn-protection notice:** a small **cyan/sky-blue rounded box** (~RGB 60,170,255) with white 8 px text: "You are immune to damage for the next 08:47 minutes. Click to end protection early."
- Overhead tags: large white serif-less name ("ECHWORLD777") with "[989]" (player number) under it in brackets. The job uniform logo (McDonald's style) floats as an icon.
