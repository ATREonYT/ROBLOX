## The overall look
- **A FiveM-style ("GTA RP server") HUD**, the most serious-RP of the pack: chat/server feed top-left, `/commands` in chat, character registration with height/weight/DOB, a driver-licence quiz, weight-limited inventory, and Rules on the loading screen.
- **V1 HUD, bottom-left:** two rows of thin **flat horizontal bars** (~4% wide each, no frame). Row 1 is a **red health bar** with a pink heart icon and an **orange hunger bar** with a fork/knife icon. Row 2 is a dark/teal armour bar with a shield and a **blue thirst bar** with a bottle icon. Money is a large **green bold "$1000"** with a teal icon. Bottom-right: a dark pill "Cash ......... $1,000" plus a wallet icon, and a "ID 21" server-ID tag in white.
- **V2 HUD, bottom-left:** a mic icon with "Regular" (voice range) and a dashed volume line, then a **red health line** with a heart. Below are **4 square status tiles** (~2.2% each, dark blue-grey rounded): shield/armour (blue), food (bowl), water (drop) and stamina (runner), each with a % under it ("0% 100% 100% 100%"). Clean flat white glyphs.
- **V2 top-right block:** the game logo (round "OFF1700 CHICAGO" sticker-style emblem in red/black, the "Welcome to Chicago" sign) or a small red round badge. A clock "16:39" and date "22.11.2026" with icons. The player row has "👤 Hoot hoot Droppin · ID 27 · 💼 Job", and money as **two small pill chips**: green cash "$5,086" and teal bank "$0". Weather top-centre: a cloud icon plus "32°F · March 17".
- **Chat/feed, top-left:** the default Roblox chat, but filled with system lines in **red/blue tags** ("[FOX 5] Anthony Jr [210] has been found unresponsive Near Unknown Area…", "[CITIZENS APP]: A shooting occurred at 12:00…", "[TWITTER] …"). The news and dispatch system lives in chat.
- **Overhead tags:** "Name [ID]" in white/orange, with "17-24 | 60" (age range | height?) under it in small white.
