# Da Hood — GUI notes

**Roblox store data (games.roblox.com, fetched 2026-11-03):** name "Da Hood" · universe 1008451066 · place 2788229376 · creator **Da Hood Entertainment** (group) · 1,895 playing at fetch time · 2,962,455,482 visits (≈3.0 B) · 3,011,972 favourites · 1,299,030 up / 543,383 down (71% like) · created 2019-01-27 · updated 2026-11-01 · genre Town and City · 45-player servers. The store text opens with "A difficult game… Account has to be at least 10 days to play." It is a PvP combat game more than an RP game.

**Videos used:** `CLqEw-kWKLk` (Kicorban Gaming, "How To Play Da Hood", 2023-09-08), `RmYw8-ipBiE` (rex, tips, 2023-07-05, Korean-language client), `w6b_9SjUjvs` (sai, "How to actually improve", 2024-04-12), `aGAPCDKuso8` (Dynamic, Halloween update 2026, 2026-10-19). The HUD has barely changed between 2023 and 2026. The 2026 frames add keybind hints and an event shop.

## The overall look (unchanged since 2023)
- **Stat bars, bottom-centre, right above the hotbar:** three thin horizontal bars side by side (~9% wide each, ~1.2% tall) with **no frame**. **Energy** is yellow-green (~RGB 170,200,40) with a yellow ⚡ glyph left of it. **Health** is bright green (~RGB 40,210,60) with a red ❤ glyph. **Armor** is steel-blue (~RGB 40,140,190) with a blue shield glyph. The labels are written **inside** the bar, right-aligned in tiny white bold with a dark outline. The bar background is dark grey.
- **Hotbar:** 5–10 small square slots (~2.7% wide) in a translucent dark grey. Item icons are **small 3D renders** (revolver, knife, cookie, shotgun, phone, burger, pizza…). Slot numbers sit top-left. The selected slot has a **2 px blue outline** (~RGB 60,120,255).
- **Money, bottom-right:** large **green** (~RGB 60,220,100) bold sans "$24,890,7…" with a small round green "+" button above it (buy-cash shortcut). Above that is the **ammo**: a row of tiny white bullet glyphs then "63" (reserve) in white.
- **Bottom-left:** a row of 5–7 tiny white icons (settings gear, crew/people, inventory, checklist, voice/mic, X, plus a red-white car/mask icon). They are icon-only, ~1.5% wide.
- **Left edge:** a small blue gun-sticker button and an "Animations / Animations++" two-row white mini panel. The emote panel slides out as a white list ("Lean, Lay, Dance1, Dance2, Greet, Chest Pump") with a **red "CLOSE"** row and a red "Enable Gun-Close Combat" row.
- **Top-right:** a semi-transparent white "Players / Wanted" leaderboard (2 columns), and under it "Officer · Respect" tabs. The Roblox core-style list is restyled in white with low opacity.
- **Interaction is world-space:** store items lie on the floor as **green neon pads** with a floating label "[High-Medium Armor] - $2440" or "[Revolver] - $1463" in white with a black outline. You click the pad to buy. There is no shop window.
- **Overhead:** a tiny icon pair (muted-mic and video-cam icons) in a rounded grey pill above heads, plus a white speech bubble with the tutorial guide's text.

**Takeaways:** Da Hood's UI is tiny, functional and almost unchanged for years. There are no shop windows: everything is bought by clicking world pads with floating "[Item] - $price" labels. The core HUD is three thin coloured bars, a 3D-icon hotbar and big green money. Menus (skins, settings) are small dark panels with coloured pill buttons and rarity colours (green/red/purple/yellow).
