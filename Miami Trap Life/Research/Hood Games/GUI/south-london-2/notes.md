## The overall look — the most "mobile-game" style of the pack
- **Money, top-right:** big **neon-green italic bold** text (~RGB 60,255,90, slight dark outline) in a short form "£572.8K", next to a **green banknote icon in a rounded box** with a small down-chevron. It is the loudest element on screen.
- **Two round status icons** under the money: **yellow ring + lightning bolt** (energy) and **red ring + heart** (health). These are fill-rings (radial meters) with glyphs, ~2.5% wide.
- **"SEASON LEVEL"** (tiny italic caps) above a **purple ring badge** with the number "1" (~RGB 170,60,220) on the right edge.
- **Quest tracker, top-left:** a yellow "!" icon, the quest title in **italic quotes, orange-red** ("'A New Beginning'", "'Passive Income'", "'Fast n' Furious'"), the task in white bold ("Earn £20 Working At The Sports Shop"), rewards "+800£" (green) and "+1600XP" (pink/magenta), a progress line "0/20", and a dark rounded **"TRACK QUEST"** / "STOP TRACKING" pill with white bold italic caps.
- **Left menu column:** icon buttons with italic white captions. **Shop** is a glossy red 3D shopping basket, **Updates** a cartoon clipboard, **Factions** a small photo-like gang thumbnail, **Settings** a grey 3D gear. A white ◀ collapse arrow sits beside them.
- **Keybind list, bottom-left:** "Tab Show Usernames · C Crouch · Shift Run" (on gun: "G Lower/UpGun · Q Give Current Tool · F Chamber Gun · R Reload · Shoot"). The tiny key letter is in a dark box, with the action in white sans.
- **No-PVP toggle, top-centre:** a dark rounded pill with shield icons on both ends, "CLICK TO DISABLE NO-PVP" in italic caps and a green "ENABLED" (or red "DISABLED") under it.
- **Fonts:** heavy italic condensed display font (like "Bangers"/"Luckiest Guy"-adjacent but sharper) for headings, plus clean sans for small labels.
