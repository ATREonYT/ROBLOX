## The overall look
- **Player card, bottom-left (2024–25 HUD):** a dark charcoal rounded rectangle (~RGB 35,35,35, ~90% opacity, ~18% of screen width × 7% tall). It holds a round headshot avatar on the left, the display name in white regular sans (Gotham-like), `@username` in **orange** (~RGB 230,120,40) under it, money in **green** (~RGB 90,220,90, "£262") bottom-left, and an in-game clock "03:16 AM" bottom-right in white.
- **Two vertical bars right of the card:** thin rounded vertical pills (~1% wide × 7% tall). **Green = health** with a hand-holding-heart emoji above it, and **orange = hunger/energy** with a brain-heart emoji above it. The emoji icons are full-colour Apple/Twemoji style.
- **"SAFE ZONE"** is written above the card in neon-green glowing graffiti/brush font (~RGB 60,255,150) when the player is inside one.
- **Hotbar, bottom-centre:** square dark-grey slots (~5% wide) with a 1-px lighter border, slot numbers top-left, and item names centred in white 9 px ("Phone", "MET AR-15", "Shotgun", "Frisk", "Taser", "Handcuffs", "Flashlight", "Police Baton"). The selected slot has a **2–3 px bright blue (~RGB 70,130,255) outline**.
- **Bottom-right:** a white gear icon (settings), the weather label "Snow", and an "X". Above it, **Twitter toasts**: a blue Twitter-bird square icon, "Twitter" in bold, then the tweet text in white regular ("who sell gold draco"). There is no background box, just a text shadow.
- **Top-centre rule ticker:** a thin white line of regular sans ("Killing people for no reason will make you fall…", "Scamming money is allowed. Be careful who you buy from."). **Server events** appear in red under it ("The BANK is currently being robbed!").
- **Top-right:** a "0/5" counter (wanted level or arrests) in white, with a small white 3D snowman/character icon under it.
- Older 2024 HUD (`IHjFyUcj1Ec`): instead of the card, there is a tiny vertical stack at the left-middle of three dark rows, each with an icon (person, red heart "100%", green cash "£0").
- Overhead name tags: a small coloured emoji-badge row ("🪐💥"), "LVL 9999" in white condensed caps, then the name in grey thin sans, and "GROUP MEMBER" in green italic for group members.
