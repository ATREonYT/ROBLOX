# Game Concepts - RP and Fun

Back to [[00 Build Router]] · Rules behind these: [[26 Fun and Roleplay Playbook]] · Town build: [[Blueprint - Roleplay Town]]

Seven original ideas for after Cannon Evolution. Each takes a proven *kind* of fun (see the ten ingredients in note 26 §2) and adds a mechanic we haven't seen on the charts. None copies a game's name, map, art or items. All are kid-safe by design and can start from `new-game-kit/`. Each one is **gameplay-first, not a hangout** (a hangout is 16+ under the 2026 rules; see [[Blueprint - Roleplay Town]] §0), and round-based ones can use kit `Rounds.luau` ([[32 Round-Based Games Playbook]]).

Score = how many of the ten fun ingredients it hits, plus build size (S = 1-2 weeks, M = 3-5, L = 6+).

## ⭐ 1. Pet Rescue Town (recommended first)
**Pitch:** a bright town where every job saves animals. Firefighters get kittens down from trees, lifeguards pull ducklings from the pond, vets heal them, everyone else adopts and raises them.
- **Fun:** roles that need each other (rescuer → vet → adopter), toys (hose, ladder, net, ambulance siren), a self-serve event every few minutes ("A puppy is stuck on the clock tower!"), a pet that follows you and grows a visible glow as you raise it.
- **Fresh twist:** rescues are **town-wide co-op moments** with a cinematic beat (camera swing, cheer sting, everyone who helped gets a "Rescuer" badge on the pet's tag). The pet you rescued remembers who saved it.
- **Long chase:** raise a pet to its Hero stage; rank up your job (Cadet → Captain).
- **Reuses:** kit Pets, EggHatch (as "adoption crates" with shown odds), Roleplay, Interact; our VFX recipes for the rescue beat.
- **Money:** pet packs, vehicle pack (pet ambulance, rescue helicopter), outfit pack, private servers.
- Ingredients 8/10 · **Size M**.

## 2. Wild Weather Town
**Pitch:** an RP town where a giant weather wheel in the plaza spins every 5 minutes: jelly rain, bouncy snow, a wind day that floats you, a sunny heatwave with water fights.
- **Fun:** gentle chaos for everyone at once (ingredient 9), jobs that respond (snowplough driver, umbrella shop, ice-cream truck on hot days), clip-worthy spins.
- **Fresh twist:** the weather changes the *physics and jobs*, not just the sky, and players vote on the next spin with coins they earned.
- Ingredients 7/10 · **Size M**.

## 3. Backyard Tiny Town
**Pitch:** players are toy-sized and live in a town built in a giant back garden. Jobs: ladybug taxi driver, ant delivery, snail bus, acorn café.
- **Fun:** a stage that tells stories by scale (a puddle is a lake, a hose is a flood event, the family cat walks through and everyone runs).
- **Fresh twist:** **giant-world events** (sprinkler rain, a football lands on the market, the lawnmower passes and the town moves house), all kid-safe and funny.
- Ingredients 7/10 · **Size L** (big art job; the scale must read instantly).

## 4. Fairground Friends
**Pitch:** each player runs a fairground ride or stall; others ride it. Rides fling, spin and splash riders (opt-in chaos), stall owners hand out prizes.
- **Fun:** roles that need each other (operator and riders), physics comedy, show-off (the busiest ride gets a crown on the map).
- **Fresh twist:** rides are **built from parts with friends** and get a ride rating from riders' screams (time in the air, spins, splashes).
- Ingredients 8/10 · **Size M**. Good cousin to Cannon Evolution's launch feel.

## 5. Prop Party (round-based)
**Pitch:** a timed theme ("Kitchen!"), everyone disguises as a prop in a room, seekers have 90 s to find them, then the whole server votes the funniest disguise onto the podium.
- **Fun:** short rounds, secrets, a creative vote (the DTI loop plus hide-and-seek), great clips.
- **Fresh twist:** **the disguise vote is the main prize**, not survival, so being found still gets laughs.
- Ingredients 8/10 · **Size S**. Fastest to ship; good first social game to learn on.

## 6. Sky Post
**Pitch:** a co-op delivery service across floating islands. Fire parcels from cannons, glide after them, deliver to other players' sky houses.
- **Fun:** toys (cannons, gliders), roles (launcher, catcher, sorter), visible ownership (your island house), a rush hour event.
- **Fresh twist:** **players' own houses are the destinations**, so deliveries carry real gifts between real players.
- **Reuses:** Cannon Evolution's launch, flight camera and landing effects directly.
- Ingredients 7/10 · **Size M**.

## 7. Food Truck Festival
**Pitch:** everyone owns a food truck. Drive to where the crowd is, cook short mini-games for NPCs and real players, and win the festival's "Best Truck" vote each round.
- **Fun:** vehicles as social objects, a crowd that moves (festival stages change), a public vote.
- **Fresh twist:** the **crowd follows events across the map**, so trucks race to the next stage.
- Ingredients 7/10 · **Size M**.

## How to pick
- **Want the biggest RP shot:** Pet Rescue Town. It has the strongest "roles that need each other" and a pet chase that kids love, and it's safe by theme.
- **Want to learn social games fast:** Prop Party first (1-2 weeks), then a town.
- **Want to reuse Cannon Evolution:** Sky Post or Fairground Friends.

Golden slice for any of them (per CLAUDE.md "golden slice first"): the spawn plaza, one job with its tool and customer, one self-serve event, the door test on 5 toys, on a phone. Show that before building the rest.
