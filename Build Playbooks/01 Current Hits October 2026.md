## The families, and what each one teaches

### Egg Run (the dominant formula right now)
Steal An Egg, Break and Steal an Egg, Fake an Egg, Climb For Animals Eggs, Bike For Egg!, Steal A Pet And Ride It!, Ride A Pet.
Same skeleton everywhere: **a treadmill or vehicle raises Speed → Speed opens biomes → grab eggs → hatch animals → animals on your base earn cash every second (also offline) → upgrade the treadmill and base → rebirth.** The variants differ only in the **verb** (steal, climb, bike, ride, fake) and the **world shape** (flat biomes, a vertical climb, a track).
- Climb For Animals Eggs (100% GRIND, Sept 2026): peak 167K, 98.7% likes; climbing replaces running. Like + join the group gives +10K Speed. ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10765490774/climb-for-animals-eggs))
- Bike For Egg! (Huron Games, Sept 2026): peak 151K, 95.2% likes; adds stealing eggs from players, sizes, mutations and an **Animal Index** with rewards. ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10766620257/bike-for-egg))
- Build it: [[Blueprint - Egg Run]].

### +1 Evolution / +1 stat
+1 Venom Evolution, +1 Anime Eyes, Bug Evolution, our own +1 Cannon Evolution. Train one number that only goes up, then **show it off** with a big visible payoff (a launch distance, a body that grows, a form that evolves). Build it: [[Blueprint - Plus One Evolution]].

### +1 Speed Escape / punch to escape
+1 Speed Keyboard Escape, Punch to Escape!. Every step adds +1 speed, and more speed makes the course harder to control, so failing is still progress. Build it: [[Blueprint - Plus One Speed Escape]].

### Roll and Place RNG
Anime Dice, Sol's RNG. Roll units, keep the best on your plot for income, and luck and income upgrades feed each other. Anime Dice: dice tiers set base luck (200x to 3,000x), letter grades multiply income (D 1.1x to Z+ 25x), and traits roll from 25.4% down to 0.006%. Auto Roll and Auto Sell come first. ([All Things How](https://allthings.how/anime-dice-roblox-how-to-progress-fast-full-guide/)) Build it: [[Blueprint - Roll and Place RNG]].

### Anomaly Shift (horror-lite jobs)
Animal Hospital (Anomaly), Sell Fries! [Anomaly], Scary Shawarma Kiosk: the ANOMALY. A normal job on a night shift where some customers or patients are subtly wrong, and you must spot them. Servers of 30, short sessions. These spike huge and fade fast (Animal Hospital fell from 358K to 35K in a month). Build it: [[Blueprint - Anomaly Shift]].

### Physics / silly verb
Throw Food For Animals, Feed A Mosquito (incremental sim, 16-minute average session), Cook the Chicken (by slapping it), Ragdoll Reality, Fling Things and People. One funny verb you can understand from the title, with a number that grows. These fit the +1 Evolution blueprint with the "launch" swapped for the verb.

### Horror obby / co-op horror
DONT SPLASH (Bacon Theory, Oct 2026: peak 116K, 95.5M visits, "Poolrooms" atmosphere), The Hollow, DOORS, Violence District, 99 Nights in the Forest. Not our lane yet, but proof that one strong atmosphere plus a one-line rule ("don't splash") works. ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10771421858/dont-splash))

## Patterns across every family
1. **The title is the tutorial.** "Verb For Animal Eggs", "+1 X Evolution", "Steal a X". A kid knows the game from the title.
2. **Speed or one stat is the gate**, and it only goes up. Money can be lost; the stat can't.
3. **The base earns while you're away**, including offline. That's the reason to come back.
4. **Sizes + mutations + an Index** make duplicates worth keeping.
5. **Weekly updates on a fixed day** (Saturday is common), plus a seasonal tag in the title (🎃 in October).
6. **Group join + like = a gift** (Speed or a chest). Keep it modest.
7. **Clones fade in weeks.** Pick a family early in its rise, add one twist you can explain in one sentence, and plan the first 4 weekly updates before launch.
