# Design reference: luxury houses, marinas, signage, towers

Research notes behind the Nov 2026 detail pass (see `lib/Lux.lua`).

## Luxury waterfront houses (Miami canal homes)
- **Massing**: L-shaped or stacked boxes. The upper floor cantilevers toward the water. Living spaces are raised above the canal (flood resilience).
- **Materials**: off-shutter / board-formed concrete plus crisp white stucco, floor-to-ceiling glass with slim black frames, wood soffits, thin dark roof slabs with deep overhangs. The street facade stays closed and private; the water side is all glass.
- **Interiors** (from the Harmony Tech reference renders): wide-plank warm wood floors, textured grey plaster walls, a waterfall-marble kitchen island with stools and a box range hood, wood upper cabinets and open shelves. Other pieces: a linear dining table with a pendant, a low sofa facing a dark-tile fireplace, a wingback chair, large abstract black/white art, potted palms, an open-tread stair with a steel stringer, and an upholstered headboard with a draped duvet.
- **Outside**: infinity pool, loungers, umbrella, outdoor kitchen, fire pit, royal palms, uplights, a private dock with seawall cap, cleats and dock lights.
- Sources: [House & Garden: Miami elevated canal house](https://www.houseandgarden.co.za/interiors/house-tours/miamis-elevated-canal-house-redefines-luxury-waterfront-living-through-climate-responsive-design-5fead635-f61a-497c-9ad3-967576d3b102), [Dwell: tropical modern, Bay Harbor Islands](https://www.dwell.com/home/stunning-tropical-modern-home-in-miamis-bay-harbor-islands-7d810621), [Dwell: Miami house with boat lift](https://www.dwell.com/article/sothebys-international-realty-miami-coastal-retreat-real-estate-ee2b41e8)

## Superyacht marinas
- **Berths**: concrete floating pontoons and double finger berths for 40–80 ft boats, with single long berths (100–590 ft) for superyachts. Every berth has a metered power + water pedestal, plus pump-out at the big berths.
- **Site**: attenuator pontoons or a breakwater for shelter, a fuel dock, gated pontoon access (card / face readers, 24h security), and waterfront dining.
- In game: wide fairways (40+ studs), numbered berths, pedestals with blue tops, lamp posts every 30, fenders along the edges, gated gangways, and most berths left empty for players.
- Source: [Marina Keppel Bay facilities](https://marinakeppelbay.com/?p=506)

## Storefront signage
- **Lightbox**: aluminium cabinet, translucent face, lit from inside.
- **Channel letters**: individual lit letters on a raceway.
- **Dimensional letters**: unlit or halo-lit.
- **Blade sign**: projects out from the facade.
- **Window lettering**: hours, OPEN neon, promos.
- **Pylon signs**: at strip malls.
- Hood shops: hand-painted wall signs (faded colour block, crooked letters, drips) and old lightboxes with dead corners and cracks.
- Sources: [MMR: 9 exterior signage options](https://mmrmagazine.com/9-exterior-signage-options/), [Yellowpop: channel letter tips](https://www.yellowpop.com/blogs/news/design-tips-for-creating-stand-out-channel-letter-storefront-signs)

## Towers
- A curtain wall reads as real when it has a mullion rhythm (about 5 studs), dark spandrel bands at every floor (about 12 studs), a few blinds half down, and a few warm lit rooms.
- Lobbies feel exclusive with an inlaid marble floor (border + medallion), a coffered ceiling with warm cove light, walnut panels with brass reveals, stone columns with brass collars, big art, and a backlit reception desk.
