# Larp Reference Analysis (49 screenshots)

The source screenshots are in `References/Larp`. This is the main visual target for Vice Bay's streets, hood and gas stations. It is reference only; nothing is copied from that game (no names, logos or assets).

## Why it reads as "real" while staying low poly
Shapes are simple boxes. The quality comes from five things done everywhere, consistently:

1. **Strong PBR textures on big flat surfaces.**
   - Cracked stucco, painted cinder block and dirty brick on walls.
   - Sidewalks are pale concrete slabs with dark water stains.
   - Asphalt is dark and worn, with brown oil and dirt blotches.
2. **Wear decals on the road.**
   - Light-edged patch repairs: a dark rectangle with a pale crumbly rim. These are everywhere and are the single most recognisable detail.
   - Big oil stains, crack lines and faded paint.
3. **Bold, clean road paint.**
   - Wide zebra crosswalks on every leg of a junction, plus stop bars.
   - Yellow diagonal-striped junction boxes and turn arrows (left/straight/right).
   - Red curb paint at corners; a green bike-lane box.
   - Orange dashed rumble strips with yellow bollards on medians.
4. **Dense urban clutter attached to buildings.**
   - Wall-mounted AC condensers (round fan grille) on every building, two or three per facade.
   - Barred windows, roll-up shutters, rusty awning bands and graffiti pieces.
   - Peeling rooftop billboards on steel frames, dumpsters ("DRY GARBAGE"), ATMs, vending machines and chain-link fences.
5. **A sky full of wires.**
   - Wooden power poles with crossarms and transformer cans, and steel lattice pylons.
   - Many sagging black cables cross the frame in almost every shot.
   - Gooseneck cobra-head street lights; traffic lights on long mast arms.

## Signature places
- **Concrete flood channel:** sloped walls with huge colourful graffiti, a centre low-flow channel, debris/trash piles, a burnt car, arched road bridges and box culverts. Trees line the top.
- **Elevated freeway overhead:** stained concrete underside, tall round columns, chain-link on the deck edge.
- **Gas station (green brand):**
  - Thick green canopy fascia on black steel truss legs.
  - Pump islands: cream base, teal-white pumps.
  - Small cream convenience store with a brand sign, a tall pylon sign ("NOW HIRING"), dumpsters and an ATM.
- **Convenience store:** brick box with a full yellow fascia sign and a tagline under it, a glass front with colourful shelves inside, and a parking lot.
- **Gun / shop buildings:** two storeys, red parapet cap, big grid windows, "SALE" posters, stone-clad corners and a shrub bed along the front.
- **Motel:** two-storey L-shape around a parking lot, an exterior walkway with railing, an external stair, AC units, a vending machine and a tall motel pylon sign.
- **Ranch houses:**
  - One storey, blue-grey asphalt-shingle hip roofs, white stucco or brown brick.
  - Window AC units, chain-link front fences, "FOR SALE" signs.
  - Big shade trees and old cars on the lawn.
- **Beach road:** palm-lined planted median with long grass, a beach with umbrellas, and modern beach houses in the distance.
- **Construction site:** an unfinished concrete frame with tarps, and a tower crane.

## Mood and lighting
There are two looks:
- **Day:** warm, slightly hazy blue sky with soft clouds, strong shadows and a little desaturation.
- **Dusk/night:** purple-blue fog, dark asphalt and warm interior glow.

Trees are big leafy broadleaf meshes (not just palms), which break up skylines.
